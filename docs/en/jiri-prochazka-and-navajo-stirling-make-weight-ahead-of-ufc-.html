Jiří Procházka and Navajo Stirling Make Weight Ahead of UFC Qatar Main Event

Both fighters hit 205 pounds on the scale in Doha ahead of Saturday’s light heavyweight clash at ABHA Arena.

Mak · Combat sports
21 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · AI-assisted · reviewed by the author
ShareTelegramFacebookXWhatsApp

Former light heavyweight champion Jiří Procházka and undefeated contender Navajo Stirling concluded their pre-fight duties with an intense staredown in Doha on Friday after both men successfully weighed in at 205 pounds. The five-round light heavyweight contest headlines UFC Fight Night 294 on Saturday, November 21, at the Ali Bin Hamad Al-Attiyah Arena (ABHA Arena) in Al Rayyan, Qatar.

Friday's faceoff set the stage for a critical meeting in the 205-pound division. Procházka (32-6-1) enters the cage ranked No. 2, returning to action for the first time since suffering a knockout defeat against Carlos Ulberg in April. Stirling (11-0), ranked No. 5, makes his first headline appearance for the promotion after recording three consecutive stoppage victories during the 2026 calendar year, most recently stopping former champion Jan Błachowicz in the opening round in August.

The City Kickboxing Dynamic

The matchup carries personal and tactical intrigue due to Stirling’s training camp at Auckland’s City Kickboxing, where he serves as a primary training partner to Ulberg. Procházka, who previously claimed the light heavyweight belt by submitting Glover Teixeira at UFC 275 in June 2022 before vacating the title following a severe shoulder injury, stated during fight week that a victory over Stirling is intended to clear a path toward a championship rematch with Ulberg.

Stirling, aged 29, earned his contract through Dana White’s Contender Series in 2024 and has built a 6-0 record inside the Octagon. During the buildup, the New Zealander pointed to Procházka’s hittable defensive style as a clear path to victory, while Procházka emphasized that he has prepared for either a fast finish or a grueling 25-minute tactical engagement.

Undercard Weigh-In Results

Eleven of the twelve scheduled bouts were finalized without issue during the morning weigh-ins. In the co-main event, former bantamweight champion Aljamain Sterling (26-5) and Argentine featherweight Kevin Vallejos (18-1) both hit the featherweight limit of 145 pounds. The main card also features former lightweight title challenger Brian Ortega (155.5 pounds) against Dan Hooker (156 pounds), flyweight contender Asu Almabayev (125.5 pounds) against former Rizin champion Kyoji Horiguchi (126 pounds), and middleweight Jared Cannonier (185 pounds) taking on Ikram Aliskerov (185.5 pounds).

The only scale issue occurred in the welterweight preliminary section, where Tahir Abdullayev weighed in at 172 pounds—one pound above the non-title welterweight limit—for his bout against Jake Matthews (170 pounds). Abdullayev was fined a percentage of his purse, and the bout will proceed at a catchweight. The event marks the UFC's second show in Qatar following its debut in November 2025.
