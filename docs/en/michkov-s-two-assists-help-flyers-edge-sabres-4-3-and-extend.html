Michkov's Two Assists Help Flyers Edge Sabres 4-3 and Extend His Point Streak to Five Games

The 21-year-old Russian set up goals by Jamie Drysdale and Christian Dvorak, whose third-period strike won the game in Philadelphia.

M
Mak · Ice hockey
18 Nov 2026 · 1 min read · AI-assisted · reviewed by the author
ShareTelegramFacebookXWhatsApp

What happened

Russian forward Matvei Michkov had two assists as the Philadelphia Flyers beat the Buffalo Sabres 4-3 at home on Tuesday 17 November 2026, extending his point streak to five games, the Associated Press reported.

Michkov set up Jamie Drysdale's goal at 6:39 of the second period, which tied the game 1-1. Owen Tippett then scored in the second period from a pass that Drysdale threaded through several Buffalo defenders.

In the third period Travis Sanheim scored 29 seconds in, his fourth goal of the season, from a cross-ice pass by Christian Dvorak. Dvorak scored the winning goal at 5:23 of the third period to make it 4-3, with Michkov providing one of the assists, RIA Novosti reported.

Tage Thompson scored his 13th goal of the season and added an assist for Buffalo, and Mattias Samuelsson and Jiri Kulich also scored for the Sabres.

The goalkeepers

Joseph Woll made 16 saves for Philadelphia. Alex Lyon made 29 saves for Buffalo.

Why it matters

The AP reported that Michkov became the first Flyers player since Simon Gagne in 2001 to put together four point streaks of five games before the age of 22. Buffalo have now gone five games without a win, with four losses in regulation time and one after it.

What happens next

Philadelphia start a three-game road trip in Ottawa on Thursday. Buffalo continue a six-game road trip, their longest of the season, in Washington on Thursday.
