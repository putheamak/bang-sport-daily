Stephen Thompson Retires With a Spinning-Kick Knockout at UFC 334

The 43-year-old ended a 16-year MMA career by stopping Charles Radtke in the third round at Madison Square Garden. Dana White said the UFC will test him for a commentary role.

What happened

Stephen "Wonderboy" Thompson ended his mixed martial arts career with a knockout win over Charles Radtke at UFC 334 at Madison Square Garden in New York on Saturday 14 November 2026. He had announced earlier in the week that it would be his last fight.

Radtke, who came in with a 13-5 record, took Thompson down in the first and second rounds. Late in the second round Thompson began to land kicks to the head and body, ESPN reported. In the third round he dropped Radtke with a spinning hook kick to the head, and the fight ended at 2 minutes 54 seconds.

Thompson, 43, finishes with a professional record of 18 wins, 9 losses and 1 draw from 28 fights. He went into the bout on a three-fight losing streak, the last of them a split-decision defeat by Gabriel Bonfim in July 2025, Yahoo Sports reported.

After the fight

Joe Rogan interviewed Thompson in the Octagon. During a highlight video of his career, Thompson, his father, his coaches and Rogan all became emotional, ESPN reported. Thompson thanked the fans and said he could not have finished his career in a better way.

At the post-event press conference, UFC CEO Dana White said the promotion would bring Thompson in and run him through a series of tests for a broadcast role. "He'd probably be really good at it," White said, as reported by HITC and Yahoo Sports. Thompson worked as a desk analyst at UFC Kansas City in 2025.

The career

Thompson grew up in Simpsonville, South Carolina, and trained at Upstate Karate, the gym run by his father, Ray Thompson. Before MMA he was unbeaten in kickboxing, with 37 amateur wins and 20 professional wins.

He made his UFC debut at UFC 143 in February 2012, when he knocked out Dan Stittgen with a head kick. He fought 23 times in the Octagon. A run of seven straight wins, including victories over Robert Whittaker and Johny Hendricks, earned him a welterweight title fight against Tyron Woodley. Their first fight, at UFC 205 in November 2016, was a majority draw, so Woodley kept the title. Woodley won the rematch at UFC 209 in March 2017 by majority decision.

Thompson's other UFC wins include victories over Rory MacDonald, Jorge Masvidal, Vicente Luque and Kevin Holland.

What happens next

White did not give a date for the test. If Thompson passes, he would join the UFC's broadcast team.
