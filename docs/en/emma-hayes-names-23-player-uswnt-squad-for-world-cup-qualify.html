Emma Hayes Names 23-Player USWNT Squad for World Cup Qualifying Campaign

A quarter-final victory over El Salvador on 27 November would seal the United States a berth at the 2027 Women's World Cup in Brazil.

M
Mak · Football
21 Nov 2026, 12:24 (GMT+7) · 2 min read · AI-assisted · reviewed by the author
ShareTelegramFacebookXWhatsApp

United States women's national team head coach Emma Hayes named her 23-player squad on Wednesday for the upcoming Concacaf W Championship, where a quarter-final victory over El Salvador on 27 November will confirm qualification for the 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup in Brazil.

The tournament serves as the primary regional qualifying event for both the 2027 World Cup and the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. All four semi-finalists earn direct passage to Brazil, while beaten quarter-finalists enter a repechage round for two berths in February's inter-confederation play-offs in Morocco. The United States have already qualified for the 2028 Games as host nation, shifting the primary focus of the campaign entirely toward the opening knockout match.

Olympic Core and Defensive Absences

Hayes included eight players from the squad that won Olympic gold in Paris, led by the forward trio of Trinity Rodman, Mallory Swanson, and Sophia Wilson. Midfielder Sam Coffey returns to international duty after missing the previous window through injury. Central defender Naomi Girma was omitted as she continues rehabilitation from persistent muscle issues.

In goal, Claudia Dickey of Seattle Reign, Mandy McGlynn of Utah Royals, and Manchester United's Phallon Tullis-Joyce will compete for the starting shirt. The position remains vacant following the retirement of long-time goalkeeper Alyssa Naeher.

Uncapped Shores Joins Balanced Roster

The selections point to a core group established after two years of squad rotation. Since taking charge, Hayes has examined 61 total players and awarded senior debuts to 33. The roster chosen for the championship averages 26.1 years of age, with only midfielders Lindsey Heaps and Rose Lavelle aged 30 or older.

Angel City defender Evelyn Shores earned her first senior international call-up, becoming only the second uncapped player in USWNT history selected for a World Cup qualifying squad, following Taylor Flint in 2022. Only three other outfield players hold fewer than 12 senior appearances: Utah Royals defender Kate Del Fava (one cap), San Diego Wave defender Kennedy Wesley (nine caps), and Kansas City Current midfielder Croix Bethune (seven caps), who scored from a direct free-kick in the NWSL playoffs last weekend.

The United States have won nine of the 11 editions of the Concacaf W Championship and enter the fixture against El Salvador having never failed to qualify for a Women's World Cup.
