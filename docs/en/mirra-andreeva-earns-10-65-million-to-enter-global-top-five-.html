Mirra Andreeva Earns $10.65 Million to Enter Global Top Five in 2026 Prize Money

The 19-year-old French Open champion finished second among female players, trailing only Kazakhstan's Elena Rybakina in Sportico's year-end rankings.

M
Mak · Tennis
24 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · AI-assisted · reviewed by the author
ShareTelegramFacebookXWhatsApp

Russian 19-year-old Mirra Andreeva generated $10.65 million in prize money during the 2026 tennis season, placing fifth across all players worldwide in financial rankings published by Sportico on November 23. The world No. 2 finished behind only Kazakhstan’s world No. 1 Elena Rybakina among female players, joining three ATP stars atop the combined tour earnings list.

Germany’s Alexander Zverev led all players in the 2026 calculations with $18.18 million, supported by men's singles titles at the French Open and the US Open. Carlos Alcaraz finished second with $12.55 million following his title run at the ATP Finals in Turin, while Wimbledon champion Jannik Sinner placed third at $11.61 million. Rybakina secured fourth overall and led the WTA Tour with $11.13 million, leaving Andreeva fifth at $10.65 million.

Roland Garros triumph anchors Andreeva's rise

Andreeva, born in Krasnoyarsk, compiled a tour-leading 55 singles victories across 71 matches during 2026. Her season featured four WTA singles titles, highlighted by her first Grand Slam singles championship at Roland Garros and an undefeated title run at the WTA Finals.

The $10.65 million purse marked a sharp acceleration in Andreeva's career on-court compensation. After collecting $25,811 in 2022, she took home $750,477 in 2023, $1.92 million in 2024, and $4.7 million in 2025 before crossing the eight-figure barrier in her second full year as a senior top-10 competitor.

Central Asian and global milestones

Rybakina's $11.13 million haul established the highest single-season prize total recorded by a tennis player representing Kazakhstan. Her season included Grand Slam victories at the Australian Open and the US Open, alongside WTA tournament titles in Stuttgart and Ningbo.

Aryna Sabalenka finished sixth overall on the combined leaderboard with $8.61 million. Ben Shelton of the United States and Linda Noskova of the Czech Republic followed in a tie for seventh at $8.29 million each. Coco Gauff ranked ninth with $7.76 million, and Jessica Pegula placed 10th with $6.47 million.

Russian men's No. 1 Daniil Medvedev finished 13th overall with $5.66 million, sitting between Ukraine's Elina Svitolina ($6.15 million), Czech finalist Karolina Muchova ($5.67 million), and Italian semifinalist Flavio Cobolli ($5.31 million). Frances Tiafoe rounded out the top 15 with $5.21 million. Andreeva and Rybakina are scheduled to open their 2027 campaigns in Australia in January as the top two seeds for the Australian Open in Melbourne.
