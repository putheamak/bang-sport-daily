Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06

This policy explains what happens to your data when you visit Bang Sport Daily ("we"). The site is run by Mak.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name or email through this site. We do not use our own cookies or analytics at the moment.

Services that receive technical data

  • Hosting: the site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, the host processes your IP address and browser information to deliver pages and keep them secure.
  • Photos: photos are loaded from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org), which receives your IP address when an image loads. See the Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy.
  • Fonts: fonts are served from our own site; we do not use Google Fonts.
  • Share buttons are plain links. Nothing is sent to social networks unless you click one.

Advertising (if enabled in the future)

If we show ads, they may be provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings or at www.aboutads.info. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent through a certified consent tool before any such cookies are used. We will update this policy before ads go live.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or similar laws) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold almost no personal data, most requests concern the services above. Contact us via the contact page.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.
