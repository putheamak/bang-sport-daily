Aleksandr Golovin and Nail Umyarov Sidelined for Russia’s November Fixtures

Head coach Valery Karpin confirms injury concerns as the national team prepares for upcoming friendly matches in Tashkent.

Mak · Football

ShareTelegramFacebookXWhatsApp

The Russian national football team faces significant personnel challenges ahead of their November international schedule, as key midfielders Aleksandr Golovin and Nail Umyarov have been ruled out of training sessions in Tashkent.

Head coach Valery Karpin confirmed on November 11 that Umyarov sustained an injury during the team’s preparation camp. The Spartak Moscow midfielder will miss the entirety of the November fixtures. Golovin, who plays for AS Monaco, also did not participate in the squad's latest pre-match training, leaving his availability for the upcoming friendlies in doubt.

Impact on the Midfield Setup

The absence of Umyarov, a consistent presence in the defensive midfield role for Spartak, forces a tactical adjustment for Karpin. Since his debut, Umyarov has been viewed as a primary option for building play from the back. His forced withdrawal requires the coaching staff to look toward alternative personnel to balance the team’s transition play against their upcoming international opponents.

Golovin’s status remains a focal point for supporters. As one of the few Russian players currently active in a top-five European league, his involvement is central to the team’s offensive structure. The team is currently based in Tashkent, where they are finalizing preparations for a series of exhibition matches intended to maintain squad cohesion despite the ongoing suspension of Russian clubs and the national team from FIFA and UEFA competitions.

Context of the November Camp

These matches serve as a critical opportunity for the Russian Football Union (RFU) to integrate younger talent and assess depth in the absence of competitive qualifiers. The loss of two established starters complicates the objective of maintaining consistent performance levels. The medical staff is expected to provide further clarity on Golovin’s recovery timeline following the conclusion of the training camp.

For the Russian side, the focus shifts to testing players who have seen limited minutes in previous gatherings. With the squad already operating in a restricted international environment, the ability to adapt to injuries has become a recurring theme for Karpin’s tenure. The team continues its training program in Uzbekistan, with the coaching staff monitoring the fitness of the remaining squad members ahead of the scheduled matches.
