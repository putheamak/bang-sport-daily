Vinícius Scores a 12-Minute Hat-Trick as Brazil Beat Singapore 7-0

Vinícius Júnior scored in the 9th, 15th and 21st minutes for his first international hat-trick, and Endrick, Estêvão and Rayan added goals at the National Stadium.

ShareTelegramFacebookXWhatsApp

What happened

Brazil beat Singapore 7-0 in a friendly at the National Stadium in Singapore on Tuesday 17 November 2026. Vinícius Júnior scored three goals in the first 21 minutes, ESPN reported.

Vinícius opened the scoring in the 9th minute from a pass by Bruno Guimarães. He scored again in the 15th minute after a move with Endrick, and completed his hat-trick in the 21st minute, finishing a move started by Guimarães and continued by Estêvão, according to Football Today. It was his first hat-trick for Brazil.

Endrick made it 4-0 in the 31st minute. After the break Estêvão scored in the 51st minute and Rayan in the 65th, and Singapore defender Raoul Suhaimi turned the ball into his own net in the 74th minute. Vinícius was substituted after the hour.

The numbers

Brazil had 60% of possession and 11 shots on target to Singapore's one, according to ESPN's match statistics. Singapore made five saves.

Why it matters

The match was only the second meeting between the two countries. Brazil won the first, in 2008. For Brazil, coached by Carlo Ancelotti, it followed a 3-2 defeat by Japan three days earlier, decided by a goal from Tatsuhiro Sakamoto in stoppage time. Vinícius scored twice in that match. Raphinha missed the Singapore game after being sent off against Japan.

Singapore, coached by Gavin Lee, lost 4-1 to Paraguay on 13 November, so the window ended with two defeats against South American opponents.
