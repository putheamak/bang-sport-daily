PFL Confirms 14-Fight Lyon Card Headlined by Lapilus vs McKee Title Bout

The December 19 event at LDLC Arena crowns an inaugural bantamweight champion in the league's final show before its 2027 rebrand.

Mak · Combat sports

ShareTelegramFacebookXWhatsApp

The Professional Fighters League finalized a 14-fight lineup for PFL Lyon on Monday, November 23, 2026, setting the slate for its return to the LDLC Arena in Lyon-Décines, France, on Saturday, December 19.

The event features five main-card bouts and nine preliminary matchups, topped by the inaugural PFL bantamweight world championship bout between French veteran Taylor Lapilus and undefeated American wrestler Mitch McKee.

Inaugural Bantamweight Crown in Lyon

Lapilus (25-4) enters the 135-pound title clash on a six-fight winning streak. The former UFC competitor and ARES FC bantamweight champion debuted in the PFL in December 2025 at the same venue in Lyon, earning a striking-based victory. He now competes for a world championship on home soil.

McKee (11-0) carries an undefeated professional mixed martial arts record into his first promotional title appearance after earlier tenures in Bellator and the Legacy Fighting Alliance. A two-time NCAA Division I All-American wrestler at the University of Minnesota, McKee trains at Kill Cliff FC in Florida under Robbie Lawler. In his PFL promotional debut in Chicago earlier in 2026, McKee defeated former Bellator champion Sergio Pettis to claim the league's No. 1 ranking at bantamweight.

Battle Meets Atangana in Middleweight Co-Main

The co-main event pairs fifth-ranked French middleweight Boris Atangana (9-0) against fourth-ranked American Bryan Battle (13-3). Atangana preserved his undefeated record in May 2026 with a first-round knockout against veteran Jared Gooden in Brussels. Battle, a former UFC fighter, earned his place in the co-feature by beating Dalton Rosta in Charlotte, North Carolina.

The main card includes three additional bouts:
- Welterweight: Abdoul Abdouraguimov (20-1) faces Andreeas Binder (12-2)
- Middleweight: Fabian Edwards meets French prospect Ilian Bouafia
- Welterweight: Oualy Tandia fights veteran Alex Lohoré

The preliminary lineup includes former NCAA standout Bryce Meredith taking on Britain's Dean Garnett at bantamweight, alongside a 135-pound matchup pitting Baris Adiguzel against former Cage Warriors titleholder Jack Cartwright.

Final Showcase Before MVP Brand Transition

The December 19 gathering marks the last event staged under the PFL brand name. Beginning January 1, 2027, the promotion will fold event operations into the Most Valuable Promotions banner following the business integration led by chief executive Nakisa Bidarian.

The early preliminary slate at LDLC Arena is scheduled to begin at 11:00 a.m. Eastern Time (5:00 p.m. local time in France), with the main card following at 3:00 p.m. Eastern Time on the ESPN App and ESPN+.
