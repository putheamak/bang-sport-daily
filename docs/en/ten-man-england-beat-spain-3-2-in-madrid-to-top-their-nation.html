Ten-Man England Beat Spain 3-2 in Madrid to Top Their Nations League Group

Kane, Konsa and White scored before Elliot Anderson's red card, and England held on to end Spain's 43-match unbeaten run.

M
Mak · Football
16 Nov 2026 · 1 min read · AI-assisted · reviewed by the author
ShareTelegramFacebookXWhatsApp

What happened

England beat Spain 3-2 at the Metropolitano in Madrid on Sunday 15 November 2026 to finish top of Group A3 in the UEFA Nations League. The result ended the world champions' unbeaten run of 43 matches, the Press Association reported.

Harry Kane gave England the lead in first-half stoppage time from the penalty spot, after Pau Cubarsi fouled him in the area. Early in the second half, Ezri Konsa headed in Declan Rice's corner in the 53rd minute and Ben White made it 3-0 four minutes later from a Morgan Rogers pass.

England played the last half hour with ten men. In the 63rd minute Elliot Anderson was sent off for bringing down Lamine Yamal when he was the last defender. Mikel Oyarzabal scored the resulting penalty two minutes later, and substitute Nico Williams turned in a Yamal cross in the 75th minute.

How England held on

Goalkeeper James Trafford, picked ahead of Jordan Pickford, made several saves in the closing stages, Sky Sports reported, including a double save from Fermín López and Oyarzabal in stoppage time.

Why it matters

The win puts Thomas Tuchel's England through to the Nations League quarter-finals as group winners, which means they will be seeded in the draw. According to Sky Sports, the draw takes place on Friday 20 November, and the quarter-finals are played over two legs in late March 2027.

Key facts

  • Score: Spain 2-3 England.
  • England goals: Kane 45' (penalty), Konsa 53', White 57'.
  • Spain goals: Oyarzabal 65' (penalty), Nico Williams 75'.
  • Red card: Elliot Anderson (England), 63'.
  • Venue: Metropolitano, Madrid.
