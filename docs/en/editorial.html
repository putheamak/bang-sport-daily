Editorial policy

Bang Sport Daily publishes news, history and athlete stories about world sport in English, Chinese and Russian.

How stories are made

  • Sources first. Each story starts from reports by established news outlets and official sources. Every story lists its sources at the end.
  • AI-assisted writing. Drafts are written with the help of artificial intelligence (Google Gemini) and adapted into Chinese and Russian. We say so on every story.
  • Human review. Mak reads every draft, checks the key facts (names, dates, scores, quotes) against the sources, and approves it before publication. Nothing is published automatically.
  • Original work. We do not copy articles. Stories are written in our own words, add context and factual analysis, and quote other outlets only briefly and with credit.
  • Up-to-date, credible sources. News stories rely on at least two sources, including a major or official outlet, published close to the event. Blogs and social media are never used as the only source. Every week we re-check all published stories and their source links, and update or correct them when facts change.
  • Journalism standards. Stories are written in the third person as news reporting. Every draft passes an automatic standards check (accuracy flags, sources, neutral language, no clickbait) before the editor can approve it.
  • Photos. We only use freely licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons, credited to their authors, or our own designed covers. We never use photos from news agencies without a licence, and we do not publish AI-generated images of real people.

What we don't do

  • No betting tips, gambling promotion or odds.
  • No gossip about athletes' private lives, health or relationships.
  • No invented quotes, statistics or authors.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it and note the correction at the end of the story. Please report errors via our contact page.
