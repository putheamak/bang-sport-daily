About Bang Sport Daily

Bang Sport Daily tells the stories of world sport — the news, the history and the people — in English, Chinese and Russian.

How our stories are written

Every story is drafted with the help of artificial intelligence, using reports from established news outlets as sources of facts. Each story lists its sources. Nothing is published until our editor, Mak, has reviewed and approved it.

Who writes Bang Sport Daily

Every story is published under the name of its editor, Mak, who reviews and approves it. Read more on the author page and in our editorial policy.

Photos

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons and are used under their free licences; each photo shows its author and licence. When no free photo exists we use a designed section cover instead.

Corrections

If you find a mistake, please tell us and we will correct it.
