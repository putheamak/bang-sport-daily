About Bang Sport Daily

Bang Sport Daily tells the stories of world sport — the news, the history and the people — in English, Chinese and Russian.

How our stories are written

Every story is drafted with the help of artificial intelligence, using reports from established news outlets as sources of facts. Each story lists its sources. Nothing is published until our editor, Mak, has reviewed and approved it.

Our columnists are pen names

Stories are signed with pen names, one for each sport. They are not real people: they are the voices of our AI-assisted writing, each focused on one beat, and all edited by Mak.
