Russian Boxing Federation Voted In as Full World Boxing Member in Panama

Delegates at the fourth annual World Boxing Congress approve full voting rights for Russia as the Olympic governing body expands ahead of LA 2028.

ShareTelegramFacebookXWhatsApp

The Russian Boxing Federation has become a full member of World Boxing following a majority vote by delegates at the international body's fourth annual Congress in Panama City on 20 November 2026.

The decision grants Russia official voting rights under World Boxing's "one country, one vote" constitutional principle at future general assemblies. Andrei Istomin, Deputy General Secretary of the Russian Boxing Federation, represented the delegation in Panama under instructions from General Secretary Alexander Besputin.

"The most important result for us was the admission of the Russian Boxing Federation into World Boxing as a full member, which the majority of countries voted for," Istomin said.

The Shift from Endorsed to Full Status

Russia's accession to full status completes an eight-month procedural transition. World Boxing initially accepted the Russian Boxing Federation's application in March 2026 as an endorsed member without voting privileges.

The vote in Panama was part of a broader expansion, with the Congress approving applications from 47 national federations to lift the governing body to 170 full voting members and one associate member.

World Boxing President Gennadiy Golovkin, the former unified world middleweight champion from Kazakhstan who oversees the federation, addressed the assembly following the votes.

"Today marked another important milestone for World Boxing and the future of our sport," Golovkin said. "The decisions made by our member federations confirm our shared commitment to putting boxers first, strengthening our organisation, and doing everything possible to ensure boxing continues to develop as a credible and respected Olympic sport. I am particularly pleased that our members supported the course we have set leading up to the 2028 Olympic Games in Los Angeles and beyond."

Governance and the Road to LA28

World Boxing was founded in April 2023 by member associations seeking an alternative international federation capable of securing the sport's long-term future in the Olympic programme following the International Olympic Committee's de-recognition of the International Boxing Association (IBA).

Full membership integrates Russia into World Boxing's governance structure, committee appointments and constitutional ballots. Russian coaching council chairman Eduard Kravtsov told Match TV that full status normalises the country's position: "We were non-full members in World Boxing, and for Russia that was unacceptable. Everything has fallen into place."

While full membership formalises Russia's standing inside the governing body, Olympic qualifying events under World Boxing oversight are scheduled to begin in 2027 as the sport prepares for the 2028 Games in Los Angeles.
