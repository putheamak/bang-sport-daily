Kamila Valieva Ruled Out of Russian Figure Skating Championships in Chelyabinsk

The 20-year-old was omitted from the preliminary entry list after doctors restricted her training workload following health issues.

M
Mak · Olympic sports
25 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · AI-assisted · reviewed by the author
ShareTelegramFacebookXWhatsApp

Kamila Valieva will not compete at the upcoming Russian Figure Skating Championships in Chelyabinsk, missing the country’s marquee domestic competition scheduled for Dec. 17–20. The 20-year-old was absent from the preliminary entry list released by the Figure Skating Federation of Russia (FFKKR) on Tuesday evening, confirming reports first published by Russian state news agencies TASS and RIA Novosti.

Valieva’s withdrawal follows an autumn disrupted by health concerns and training restrictions. In late October, medical staff advised the skater to suspend jumping elements for a month. While Valieva resumed on-ice practice on Nov. 12, team insiders told RIA Novosti that she has yet to return to full-intensity training routines required for national-level competition.

Medical setback halts autumn schedule

The training halt forced Valieva to skip the entire Russian Grand Prix circuit this season. She withdrew from the opening stage in Kazan on Oct. 17–18 due to illness and subsequently missed the series finale in Omsk on Nov. 14–15.

Valieva made her initial competitive return in January 2026 at the Russian jumping championships after completing a four-year suspension imposed by the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) for a positive trimetazidine test dating back to December 2021. She skated in the national test skates in Moscow in September, but she has not contested a full two-program competitive event since the ban expired.

Coaching transition and eligibility shifts

The 2026–27 season has brought considerable structural changes for Valieva. After spending more than a year in Svetlana Sokolovskaia’s training group, she returned this autumn to the Khrustalny rink to work once again with veteran coach Eteri Tutberidze.

Her international status remains blocked. Although the International Skating Union (ISU) granted Valieva neutral athlete status in August, the governing body rescinded that designation in September.

Domestic figures acknowledged that Valieva’s absence represents a substantial blow to the tournament in Chelyabinsk. Renowned coach Alexander Zhulin told RIA Novosti that "the Russian championship will lose in spectacle due to her absence," while 1988 Olympic ice dance champion Natalia Bestemianova told TASS that Valieva "knows what she is doing, she has been through a lot" and must prioritize her health.

While Valieva sits out, the FFKKR’s preliminary field in Chelyabinsk features Beijing 2022 Olympic silver medalist Alexandra Ignatova (formerly Trusova) in the women's event, alongside a men's field headlined by Petr Gumennik and Vladislav Dikidzhi.
