Vietnam Earn Historic First U17 World Cup Point in 3-3 Thriller Against New Zealand

Cristiano Roland's squad fought back three times before Trương Nguyễn Duy Khang headed a 92nd-minute equalizer in Doha.

Mak · Football

ShareTelegramFacebookXWhatsApp

Vietnam’s under-17 men's national football team earned their first point in FIFA U-17 World Cup history on Monday, November 23, 2026, rallying three separate times to secure a 3-3 draw against New Zealand at the Aspire Zone in Doha, Qatar.

Substitute Trương Nguyễn Duy Khang scored the decisive equalizer in the 92nd minute, heading home after New Zealand goalkeeper Joseph Chalabi parried an initial delivery into the penalty area. The Oceania champions nearly snatched victory two minutes later when midfielder Joe McIntyre struck the goalpost from distance in the 94th minute.

Three deficits erased in Doha

New Zealand opened the scoring in the 19th minute of the Group G fixture when forward Jack Clegg converted from close range following a low cross from Matias Nunez. The Vietnamese side trailed 1-0 at half-time despite registering several dangerous attempts on goal.

Head coach Cristiano Roland’s side leveled four minutes into the second half. Midfielder Chu Ngọc Nguyễn Lực curled a direct free-kick from the left wing into the bottom corner in the 49th minute. New Zealand restored their advantage three minutes later when James Wenceslao delivered a cross from the right flank, allowing John Woldegiorgis to head past Vietnamese goalkeeper Lý Xuân Hòa in the 52nd minute.

Vietnam equalized again in the 58th minute. Nguyễn Lực split the New Zealand defense with a through ball to Lê Trọng Đại Nhân, who finished past Chalabi. The assistant referee initially raised an offside flag, but Vietnam's coaching staff requested a review under FIFA's Football Video Support (FVS) protocol, prompting the referee to overturn the call and award the goal. New Zealand capitalized on a set piece to take their third lead in the 66th minute when captain Ethan Dyer powered in a header from a corner kick.

First point keeps qualification alive

Duy Khang’s stoppage-time header secured Vietnam's breakthrough point at a global FIFA tournament following their opening 3-2 defeat against European runners-up Belgium on November 20. New Zealand also picked up their first point of the campaign after losing 3-0 to Mali in their opener.

The result leaves Vietnam third in Group G with one point and a minus-one goal difference. Mali top the standings with six points after defeating Belgium, who sit second on three points. New Zealand occupy fourth place with one point and a minus-three goal difference. Under the 48-team tournament structure in Qatar, the top two teams in each group advance to the round of 32 alongside the eight best third-placed finishers.

Vietnam finish their group schedule on Thursday, November 26, 2026, when they face Mali at the Aspire Zone.
