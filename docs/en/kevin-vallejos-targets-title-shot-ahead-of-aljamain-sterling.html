Kevin Vallejos Targets Title Shot Ahead of Aljamain Sterling Clash at UFC Qatar

The Argentine contender says defeating the former bantamweight king in Doha should secure a championship date with Alexander Volkanovski.

M
Mak · Combat sports
21 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · AI-assisted · reviewed by the author
ShareTelegramFacebookXWhatsApp

Argentine contender Kevin Vallejos stated on Saturday, November 21, 2026, that defeating former bantamweight champion Aljamain Sterling at UFC Fight Night in Doha, Qatar, should immediately earn him a featherweight title fight against Alexander Volkanovski. The two square off in the co-main event at the Ali Bin Hamad Al-Attiyah Arena.

The Stakes at Featherweight

Vallejos enters the cage ranked inside the featherweight top 10 on a seven-fight professional winning streak. The 24-year-old from Mar del Plata holds an 18-1 career record, having gone 4-0 under the UFC banner following stoppage victories that included a first-round finish over veteran Josh Emmett. His only career defeat came against Brazilian contender Jean Silva during the 2023 edition of Dana White's Contender Series.

"Fighting a guy who has already held the belt, who knows what it feels like to be a champion, is a good experience," Vallejos told MMA Fighting. "I think that if I beat him, I'm at the champion's level. He's one of the best, a legend."

Volkanovski's Stance and the Title Race

Sterling, 37, carries a 26-5 record and is ranked No. 4 in the 145-pound division. After losing his bantamweight crown to Sean O'Malley in August 2023, the New Yorker moved up a weight class, compiling a 3-1 record at featherweight with decision victories over Calvin Kattar, Brian Ortega, and Youssef Zalal.

Volkanovski, who defended his belt against Movsar Evloev at UFC 333 in October 2026, told reporter Ariel Helwani that a victory for Sterling over Vallejos would elevate the veteran above Silva in line for the next championship defence. However, Vallejos maintains that halting Sterling's momentum should redirect that title opportunity to him instead.

Clash of Grappling and Knockout Power

The co-main event pits contrasting fighting styles against each other on the undercard of the light heavyweight headliner between Jiří Procházka and Navajo Stirling. Vallejos has earned 13 of his 18 career victories by knockout, whereas Sterling relies on an extensive collegiate wrestling and submission background.

Sterling addressed the clash of styles during fight week in Doha, arguing his physical strength will pose an insurmountable puzzle for the Argentine. "He's a smaller guy," Sterling told MMA Sucka. "I'm a big wrestler, strong wrestler, and he's got his work cut out for him."
