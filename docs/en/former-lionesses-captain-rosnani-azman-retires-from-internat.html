Former Singapore Captain Rosnani Azman Retires from International Football

The 29-year-old defender steps aside after 58 caps, an overseas career in Japan and Australia, and over a decade of service to Singapore.

M
Mak · Football
25 Nov 2026 · 1 min read · AI-assisted · reviewed by the author
ShareTelegramFacebookXWhatsApp

Former Singapore women’s national team captain Siti Rosnani Azman announced her retirement from international football on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, concluding more than a decade of senior service to the Lionesses.

The 29-year-old defender accumulated 58 international caps during her tenure, anchoring Singapore’s back line across multiple regional tournaments. She featured at several editions of the Southeast Asian Games, including the 2023 tournament in Cambodia, and competed at the 19th Asian Games held in Hangzhou, China. She wore the armband in a 2-1 win over Laos in May 2023 and was given the permanent captaincy before a friendly against Pakistan that July.

“Representing Singapore has been one of the greatest honours of my football journey,” Rosnani said in a statement released by the Football Association of Singapore. “Wearing the national colours, leading my teammates as captain and sharing so many memorable moments with the team are experiences I will always be grateful for.”

A Trailblazing Overseas Path

Rosnani's international recognition developed alongside a pioneering club career abroad. After earning the inaugural FAS Women’s Premier League Player of the Year award in 2017, she moved to Australia to play for South West Queensland Thunder in the National Premier Leagues Women.

In September 2021, she made history as the first Singaporean female player to sign a professional contract in Japan when she joined WE League club INAC Kobe Leonessa. She later featured for Kibi International University Charme in Japan's Nadeshiko League before returning to Singapore's domestic circuit in 2023.

Her experience in Japan and Australia provided the Lionesses with rare overseas top-flight pedigree during a transitional phase for women's football across Southeast Asia.

Domestic Silverware

Back on the local circuit, Rosnani joined Albirex Niigata Singapore (later rebranded as FC Jurong), helping the side secure the Women’s Premier League championship in 2025. In 2026, she anchored the club’s defense during its inaugural appearance in the Asian Football Confederation’s Women’s Champions League.

Her international chapter is closed, but her club career goes on with FC Jurong.
