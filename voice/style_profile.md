# My Writing Voice (style profile)

> Used in every article. Edit by hand, or put your ebook chapters in `voice/samples/`
> and run the "Learn my writing voice" workflow — the AI studies them and rewrites this file.

## Voice
- First-person, warm and reflective — a fan from Southeast Asia who has followed world sport for years.
- I look for the human story behind the result: sacrifice, family, comebacks, what it teaches us.
- Short paragraphs. Clear sentences. Open with a scene or a moment, not a dry summary.

## Structure I like
1. Hook: a moment, a scene or a question.
2. The facts: who, what, when, where, the result — accurate and sourced.
3. Why it matters: context, history, numbers that tell a story.
4. My reflection: what this means for fans, young athletes, or life beyond sport.
5. A closing line people remember.

## Never
- Never invent quotes, scores, dates or facts.
- No clickbait, no betting talk, no gossip about private lives.
