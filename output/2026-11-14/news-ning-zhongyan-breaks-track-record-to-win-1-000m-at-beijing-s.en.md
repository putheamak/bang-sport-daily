# Ning Zhongyan Breaks Track Record to Win 1,000m at Beijing Speed Skating World Cup

*The Olympic champion delivers a dominant home-ice victory on opening day at the Ice Ribbon.*

**News** · 2026-11-14

## A Record-Breaking Season Opener in Beijing

Speed skater Ning Zhongyan claimed the men's 1,000-meter gold medal while breaking the venue track record at the ISU Speed Skating World Cup opener in Beijing on November 13, 2026. Competing at the National Speed Skating Oval, widely known as the Ice Ribbon, Ning delivered a commanding performance in his first international appearance of the new season.

The victory gave the host nation an immediate highlight on the opening day of competition. Alongside teammate Han Mei, who also featured in the national squad's opening-day entries, Ning entered the Beijing leg aiming to establish early-season momentum on home ice. The performance delighted local spectators who packed the stands of the capital venue to witness the world-class races.

## Building on Olympic Heritage

The triumph at the Ice Ribbon reinforces Ning's status among the elite competitors on the global speed skating circuit. Having established his reputation through previous international campaigns and Olympic success, the Chinese star approached the new season with clear ambitions to perform at his highest level in familiar surroundings.

Sports analysts and national coaching staff noted that opening a competitive campaign with a venue record demonstrates exceptional physical preparation during the off-season. The National Speed Skating Oval, constructed for the 2022 Winter Olympics, continues to yield fast times and competitive racing fields during major international events.

## Next Stops on the World Cup Circuit

Following the conclusion of the opening weekend in Beijing, athletes on the speed skating circuit turn their attention to subsequent rounds of the international calendar. Skaters will travel to upcoming World Cup stops across different global venues as they accumulate qualifying points and test their speed against world-class rivals ahead of continental and global championships later in the season.
