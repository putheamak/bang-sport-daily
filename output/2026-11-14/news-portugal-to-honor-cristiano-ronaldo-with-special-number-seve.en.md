# Portugal to Honor Cristiano Ronaldo with Special Number Seven Shirts Against Denmark

*Every starting player will wear the iconic jersey in a UEFA Nations League tribute following the forward's international retirement.*

**News** · 2026-11-14

## A Unified Gesture in Lisbon

When Portugal host Denmark on Saturday, 14 November 2026, every starting player for the home side will take to the pitch wearing the number seven shirt. According to reports from *A Bola* and Russian agency RIA Novosti, the tribute is designed to honour Cristiano Ronaldo, who announced the end of his international career on 5 November 2026.

The initiative was finalized during a Thursday meeting between Portuguese Football Federation (FPF) president Pedro Proença, national team coach Jorge Jesus, and senior players Bernardo Silva, Rúben Dias, and Bruno Fernandes. In addition to the pre-match shirt tribute, UEFA has approved a planned symbolic pause on the seventh minute of the match. Furthermore, the squad decided to leave the room traditionally occupied by Ronaldo at the team's training camp empty throughout the November international window.

## Context and Recent Tensions

Saturday's fixture marks the penultimate round of the UEFA Nations League group stage, with kickoff scheduled for 22:45 Moscow time. The decision to honour the veteran forward arrives after a turbulent period for the national setup. In late September, Ronaldo spent the entirety of a 2-1 victory over Norway on the substitutes' bench. He subsequently departed the team's camp following a press conference held by coach Jorge Jesus, during which the manager indicated the forward would not feature in the immediate plans.

Ronaldo later cited a breakdown in trust with the coaching staff while issuing an apology to his teammates and supporters. Although the FPF initially explored plans for an official farewell match, subsequent reports indicated that the player declined a dedicated send-off appearance. The unusual pre-match tribute and the seventh-minute applause underline the complicated yet monumental legacy he leaves behind in the red and green shirt as Portugal navigate the crucial closing stages of their group campaign.
