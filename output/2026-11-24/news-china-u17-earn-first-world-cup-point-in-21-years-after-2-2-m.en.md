# China U17 Earn First World Cup Point in 21 Years After 2-2 Morocco Draw

*Striker Zhao Songyuan scored twice in Doha before a 99th-minute equalizer denied Satoshi Ukishima's side victory.*

**News** · 2026-11-24

The China under-17 national team earned their first point at the FIFA U-17 World Cup since 2005 after a 2-2 draw against Morocco in Doha on Monday, November 23, 2026. Striker Zhao Songyuan scored twice to give China a 2-0 lead before Morocco mounted a late comeback, sealing the draw with an equalizer in the ninth minute of second-half stoppage time.

## Zhao ends 21-year goal drought

China entered the Group H fixture under pressure following a 5-0 loss to Spain in their tournament opener on November 20. Head coach Satoshi Ukishima made one change to his lineup, starting Pan Chaowei, and his side took the lead inside two minutes. Zhao intercepted a clearance in Morocco's half, drove past two defenders into the penalty area, and slid a low shot into the bottom corner. The strike was China's first goal at a men's U-17 World Cup in 21 years, dating back to Yang Xu's quarter-final goal against Turkey at Peru 2005.

Morocco, who reached the quarter-finals at both the 2023 and 2025 editions and brought a squad featuring 14 players based at European clubs, struggled to break down China's organized lines before the interval. In the 57th minute, China doubled their advantage on the counter-attack. Winger He Sifan delivered a cross from the right flank, allowing the 1.94-metre Zhao to arrive inside the six-yard box and turn the ball home for his second goal of the match.

## Morocco's stoppage-time equalizer

The momentum shifted in the 64th minute when Chinese defender Nan Zixun committed a pulling foul inside the penalty area. Morocco forward Ziyad Baha converted the spot-kick to reduce the deficit to 2-1. Morocco subsequently pinned China back into their own half, culminating in the 99th minute when Loghmani turned home a close-range rebound to level the score.

"I was very happy to help the team with two goals, but we could not hold onto our advantage," Zhao said after the match. "Conceding at the very end was really regrettable". Ukishima gathered the squad on the pitch immediately following the final whistle. "The players executed the plan well against individual quality, but we dropped into a defensive mindset too early and lost concentration at the end," Ukishima said.

## Fiji finale decides Group H fate

The result leaves Spain atop Group H with six points after a 15-0 victory over Fiji on Monday. Morocco sit second on four points with a plus-six goal difference, while China are third with one point and a minus-five goal difference. Fiji remain fourth with zero points and 21 goals conceded across two matches.

With the expanded 48-team tournament featuring 12 groups, the top two finishers in each pool and the eight best third-placed teams qualify for the Round of 32. China conclude their Group H schedule against Fiji on Thursday, November 26, in Doha, where a victory is required to keep their knockout qualification hopes alive.
