# Morocco Deny China Historic U17 World Cup Win With 99th-Minute Equalizer

*Zhao Songyuan struck twice before Rami Lougmani rescued a point for Morocco in stoppage time.*

**News** · 2026-11-24

China's under-17 national team secured their first World Cup point in 21 years on Monday, 23 November, but suffered late heartbreak in a 2-2 draw against Morocco at Aspire Zone Pitch 2 in Doha, Qatar.

Zhao Songyuan scored twice to put China 2-0 ahead before Morocco mounted a furious second-half fightback, capped by Rami Lougmani's equalizer in the ninth minute of stoppage time.

## Early Blitz and Moroccan Fightback

China entered the Group H fixture seeking redemption after opening their campaign with a 5-0 defeat to Spain on 20 November. Head coach Satoshi Ukishima instructed his side to press aggressively from the opening whistle, and the approach yielded immediate results.

In the second minute, 17-year-old forward Zhao dispossessed Moroccan defender Ismail El Aoud in the attacking third, stepped inside the penalty area, and fired a low left-footed drive into the corner. The goal marked China's first in a men's FIFA tournament since the 2005 Under-17 World Cup in Peru.

China doubled their advantage in the 57th minute. Midfielder He Sifan delivered a low cross from the right flank, allowing Zhao to slide in at the near post and turn the ball home for his second of the night. FIFA officials named Zhao player of the match after the final whistle.

Morocco, who had defeated Fiji 6-0 in their opener, responded by shifting into an all-out attack. In the 64th minute, referee Adham Makhadmeh awarded a penalty after Chinese defender Nan Zixun pulled down Ziyad Baha inside the box. Baha stepped up and calmly converted from the spot to halve the deficit.

## Heartbreak in the 99th minute

Morocco dominated possession with 69 percent and outshot China 21 to 14 over the 90 minutes. China goalkeeper Jiang Yuhan made seven saves as his defense withstood wave after wave of crosses. 

However, in the 99th minute, a scramble in the Chinese six-yard box saw the ball bounce loose to substitute Lougmani, who bundled a close-range finish inside the near post.

"The players executed the plan very well, especially during the first half," Ukishima said. "Unfortunately, in the second half, we fell into a passive defensive mindset too early, which led to the goals we conceded."

Morocco manager Ahmed Kantari praised his substitutes for altering the tempo. "I wanted fresh players with energy to come on and play differently," Kantari said. "It worked well because we created a lot of chances at the end."

## Knockout Math and Fiji Decider

The result leaves Group H tightly poised after two rounds. Spain lead with six points after defeating Fiji 15-0 on Sunday. Morocco sit second with four points, China have one point, and Fiji are bottom with zero.

Under the 48-team tournament format, the top two teams in each group and the eight best third-placed teams reach the round of 32. China conclude their group campaign against Fiji on Thursday, 26 November, while Morocco face Spain. A victory over Fiji would give China four points, which has historically guaranteed progression as a third-placed qualifier.
