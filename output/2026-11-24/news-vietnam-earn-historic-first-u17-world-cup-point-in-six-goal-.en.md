# Vietnam Earn Historic First U17 World Cup Point in Six-Goal Thriller Against New Zealand

*Truong Nguyen Duy Khang heads home in the 92nd minute as the debutants fight back three times in Doha.*

**News** · 2026-11-24

Vietnam secured their first-ever point at a FIFA U-17 World Cup on Monday, November 23, coming from behind three times to draw 3-3 with New Zealand in Doha, Qatar.

Substitute Truong Nguyen Duy Khang headed home a 92nd-minute equalizer to secure the result for head coach Cristiano Roland's team in their second Group G match at the 48-team tournament. The draw keeps Vietnam in contention for a place in the round of 32 ahead of their final group fixture against Mali on Thursday.

## Three deficits erased in Doha thriller

New Zealand opened the scoring in the 19th minute when Jack Clegg tapped in a low cross from Matias Nunez. Vietnam equalized four minutes into the second half as midfielder Chu Ngoc Nguyen Luc curled a direct free-kick past goalkeeper Joseph Chalabi in the 49th minute.

The Junior All Whites regained the lead in the 52nd minute when John Woldegiorgis headed in a delivery from James Wenceslao. Vietnam answered six minutes later; Nguyen Luc played a through ball to Le Trong Dai Nhan, who slotted home in the 58th minute. The goal was initially flagged for offside before being awarded following a Football Video Support (FVS) review.

New Zealand captain Ethan Dyer restored his side's advantage in the 66th minute by bundling the ball into the net from a set piece. With defeat looming in stoppage time, Duy Khang met a rebounded delivery inside the six-yard box in the 92nd minute to level the match at 3-3.

Clegg received FIFA Player of the Match honors, while New Zealand produced 13 attempts on goal to Vietnam's 10. The Oceania Football Confederation noted that New Zealand remain without a victory at the U17 World Cup since 2019.

## Group G standings and qualification path

The result marked Vietnam's first point in the history of the men's U17 World Cup, following their opening 3-2 defeat to European runners-up Belgium on November 20, where Le Sy Bach and Nguyen Luc were on target.

In Monday's earlier Group G fixture, Mali defeated Belgium 2-1 to secure first place with six points and guaranteed qualification. Belgium sit second with three points. Vietnam hold third place with one point and a minus-one goal difference (five goals scored, six conceded), while New Zealand sit fourth with one point and a minus-three goal difference.

The top two teams from each group, along with the eight best third-placed teams across the 12 groups, advance to the round of 32. Vietnam face group leaders Mali on Thursday, November 26, while New Zealand play Belgium.
