# Tian Bingyi Appointed China Badminton Head Coach for Los Angeles 2028 Cycle

*The 63-year-old former world champion takes the helm as two-time Olympic gold medallist Zhang Nan joins the national coaching setup.*

**News** · 2026-11-24

BEIJING — The Chinese Badminton Association (CBA) appointed veteran tactician Tian Bingyi as head coach of the Chinese national badminton team on Tuesday, November 24, 2026, placing the 63-year-old in charge of the country's preparations for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympic Games.

The decision was formally announced during the national squad's winter training mobilization conference in Beijing. Alongside Tian's appointment, the CBA confirmed that two-time Olympic champion Zhang Nan has joined the national coaching setup after serving as head coach of the Beijing municipal women's badminton team.

## A unified helm for the road to Los Angeles
Tian's appointment marks a structural return to a unified head coaching role across China's national badminton program. In April 2017, the national setup had removed the singular head coach and deputy head coach titles, dividing technical leadership between singles head coach Xia Xuanze and doubles head coach Zhang Jun.

By installing Tian at the summit of the coaching hierarchy ahead of the 2028 Olympic cycle, the CBA aims to consolidate tournament preparations across all five disciplines. Tian brings decades of experience overseeing China's first team, second team, and junior squads through multiple Olympic Games, Asian Games, and World Championship campaigns.

## Decades of championship pedigree
Born in Wuhan in July 1963, Tian built a distinguished career on the court before moving into coaching. Partnering Li Yongbo in men's doubles, Tian captured consecutive BWF World Championship titles in 1987 and 1989, earned bronze at the 1992 Barcelona Olympics, and helped China lift three straight Thomas Cup trophies in 1986, 1988, and 1990.

Following his competitive retirement after the 1992 Games, Tian took charge of China's national women's doubles group, mentoring Ge Fei and Gu Jun to back-to-back Olympic gold medals at Atlanta 1996 and Sydney 2000. He served as deputy head coach of the national team from 1997 to 2017 and was elected vice president of the Chinese Badminton Association in August 2023.

## Olympic champion Zhang Nan bolsters staff
The coaching overhaul also integrates the elite experience of 36-year-old Zhang Nan. Zhang won Olympic gold in mixed doubles with Zhao Yunlei at London 2012 and men's doubles gold alongside Fu Haifeng at Rio 2016.

During his tenure as head coach of Beijing's women's team, Zhang guided the regional squad to titles and runner-up finishes at national tournaments. His transition into the senior national staff provides Tian with contemporary championship expertise as China begins its winter training block aimed at the 2028 Games.
