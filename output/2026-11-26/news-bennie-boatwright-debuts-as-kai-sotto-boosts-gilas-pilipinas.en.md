# Bennie Boatwright Debuts as Kai Sotto Boosts Gilas Pilipinas for FIBA Window 5

*Tim Cone unveils a towering frontline in Malaysia as the Philippines targets crucial Group E wins against Syria and Iran.*

**News** · 2026-11-26

Gilas Pilipinas finalized its preparations on Thursday in Seremban, Malaysia, ahead of Friday's 2027 FIBA Basketball World Cup Asian Qualifiers meeting with Syria at Karisma Arena. Head coach Tim Cone will hand 6-foot-10 naturalized forward Bennie Boatwright his international debut, pairing him alongside 7-foot-3 center Kai Sotto in an expanded frontline for the penultimate qualifying window.

## A towering frontline for Seremban

Sotto, the 24-year-old center playing for Altiri Chiba in Japan's B.League, averaged 15.5 points and 15.0 rebounds per game during the fourth window in August 2026, when the Philippines defeated Jordan and Iran at the Mall of Asia Arena. Sotto confirmed during an online press conference on Tuesday that he is cleared to play despite managing knee swelling from his club campaign.

"Ako gusto ko lagi may kasama akong malaki na makakasama ko sa court," Sotto told reporters, noting that Boatwright's outside shooting allows the frontline to stretch the floor without losing size. Gilas also features 6-foot-10 AJ Edu, 6-foot-9 Quentin Millora-Brown, and 6-foot-8 Mike Phillips, with Kevin Quiambao and Troy Rosario bolstering the forward rotation.

## Crash course in Cone's triangle

Boatwright obtained his Philippine passport in October 2026, stepping into the naturalized player role following an injury to veteran Justin Brownlee. The 30-year-old former USC forward arrived in Seremban on November 22 after completing his stint with the Fujian Sturgeons in the Chinese Basketball Association. Gilas tested the combination on Tuesday, defeating Malaysian club KL Hornbills in a tune-up game.

"It's complicated," Boatwright said of Cone's triangle offense. "You have to know reads. It's not just a simple call and play... but my teammates are making it very easy on me." Former San Miguel teammate CJ Perez helped guide Boatwright through the sets alongside backcourt regulars Scottie Thompson, Dwight Ramos, Chris Newsome, and Juan Gomez de Liaño.

## The qualification race in Group E

The Philippines enters Window 5 sitting fifth in Group E with a 4-4 record (12 points), chasing Australia (8-0), New Zealand (6-2), Iran (5-3), and Jordan (5-3), while Syria stands at 2-6. The top three teams in each group, alongside the best fourth-placed team across both Asian qualifying pools, qualify for the 2027 FIBA Basketball World Cup in Qatar.

"What happens on paper doesn't necessarily translate to the floor," Cone said. "That's why you have practice and you work with the guys on the floor." Gilas faces Syria on Friday, November 27, and meets Iran on Sunday, November 29, before closing the qualifying schedule on home soil in Cebu in February 2027.
