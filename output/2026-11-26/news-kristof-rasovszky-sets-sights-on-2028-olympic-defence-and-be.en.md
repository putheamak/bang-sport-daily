# Kristóf Rasovszky Sets Sights on 2028 Olympic Defence and Beijing Return

*The reigning Olympic marathon swimming champion has overcome knee surgery, targeting Budapest 2027, Los Angeles 2028, and China's 2029 World Aquatics Championships.*

**News** · 2026-11-26

Olympic marathon swimming champion Kristóf Rasovszky has resumed full training following knee surgery, setting his sights on defending his men's 10-kilometre title at the 2028 Los Angeles Games and seeking an eventual return to competition in China at the 2029 World Aquatics Championships in Beijing. Speaking in Budapest on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, the 29-year-old Hungarian told Xinhua that his hunger for competition remains undiminished after navigating an 18-month sequence of physical setbacks.

## Knee Surgery and the Path to Budapest 2027

In autumn 2026, Rasovszky underwent surgery to remove two loose pieces of cartilage from his knee to prevent recurrent joint issues from disrupting his preparation for the Los Angeles Games. He returned to the pool less than three weeks after the operation and currently logs training distances averaging 10,000 metres per session, primarily in indoor facilities before transitioning to open water ahead of major meets.

"There have been ups and downs in my career. In a way, this low point came at the right time, between two Olympic Games," Rasovszky told Xinhua. "I believe I am capable of more. Actually, I don't just believe it—I know I am capable of it."

His primary qualification target is the 2027 World Aquatics Championships in Budapest. Under World Aquatics qualification criteria, the top three finishers in the men's 10km event in Budapest will secure automatic quota spots for the 2028 Olympic Games. Rasovszky secured his qualification for Paris under a similar format by taking silver at the 2023 World Championships in Fukuoka, Japan. "Representing my country at a world championship on home soil is an extra motivation in itself," Rasovszky said.

## Chasing Longevity and a Chinese Return

Rasovszky claimed silver in the 10km event at the Tokyo Olympics in 2021 before taking gold in the Seine at Paris 2024. He also won 5km gold at the 2019 World Championships in Gwangju and 10km gold at the 2024 World Championships in Doha. When the Los Angeles Games open in 2028, he will be 31, but he dismissed concerns about age by pointing to Greece's Spyridon Gianniotis, who won Olympic silver at Rio 2016 at age 36.

"As I get older, I get faster," Rasovszky said. "If I put in the same amount of work, or perhaps do it in a smarter and better way, I will probably be able to achieve the same result."

The Hungarian also highlighted his competitive history in China, where he won a FINA Open Water Swimming World Cup 10km leg at Qiandao Lake in Chun'an, Zhejiang Province, in 2017. With Beijing scheduled to stage the 2029 World Aquatics Championships, Rasovszky left the door open to competing there at age 32, while noting that the ongoing relocation of World Aquatics headquarters from Lausanne to Budapest could also offer post-retirement administrative avenues in aquatic sports.
