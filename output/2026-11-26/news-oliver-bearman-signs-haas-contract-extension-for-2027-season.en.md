# Oliver Bearman Signs Haas Contract Extension for 2027 Season

*The 21-year-old Briton completes the 22-car Formula 1 grid ahead of the Qatar Grand Prix after weeks of contract delays.*

**News** · 2026-11-26

Oliver Bearman will remain with Haas for the 2027 Formula 1 season after signing a contract extension ahead of the Qatar Grand Prix at the Lusail International Circuit on Thursday, November 26, 2026. The agreement fills the 22nd and final vacant seat on the 2027 grid across Formula 1's 11 constructors, pairing the 21-year-old British racer with Brazilian rookie Rafael Câmara.

The confirmation resolved weeks of paddock debate, coming four days after Bearman admitted at the Las Vegas Grand Prix that he was "quite disappointed" to reach round 20 of the 2026 championship without signed paperwork. Bearman has served as Haas' primary points scorer this season, collecting 23 of the American outfit's 31 championship points to help the team hold seventh place in the Constructors' Championship ahead of Audi.

## Outperforming expectations and anchoring Haas

Bearman secured his full-time Haas seat for the 2025 campaign after making three substitute Grand Prix appearances in 2024. He debuted at age 18 for Ferrari at the 2024 Saudi Arabian Grand Prix as an emergency replacement for Carlos Sainz, followed by two relief drives for Haas in Baku and São Paulo. During his first full season in 2025, Bearman finished fourth at the Mexico City Grand Prix, matching the best race result in Haas team history.

Across 48 career Formula 1 starts leading into the Qatar weekend, Bearman has recorded 71 points. Following an intra-team shakeup that saw Haas part ways with Esteban Ocon earlier in 2026, the British driver emerged as the team's operational leader.

"Continuing with Ollie into 2027 was a natural decision for us," Haas team principal Ayao Komatsu said in Thursday's announcement. "We've seen his growth, his commitment, and the quality he brings to the team, both on and off the track. He has a tremendous work ethic and a strong desire to develop both himself and the team, and there is no better person, in my opinion, to lead this team forward."

## The Ferrari link and the 2027 grid

Bearman's contract required alignment between Haas and Ferrari, whose Driver Academy the Briton joined in 2021. Ferrari supplies Haas with power units and technical support, and incoming teammate Câmara also emerged from the Scuderia's junior pipeline. With Charles Leclerc under a long-term agreement and Lewis Hamilton contracted through 2027 at Maranello, the Haas extension gives Bearman stability while maintaining his link to Ferrari's future plans.

"This team has played an integral role in my development, and I've felt a huge amount of belief and support from everyone here," Bearman said. "We've got big ambitions to keep pushing forward, and I'm excited to lead that work, building on what we've achieved, and striving for even more together – we have unfinished business."

Haas' confirmation concludes all driver market movements for 2027, locking in all 22 race seats before the final two rounds of 2026 in Qatar and Abu Dhabi.
