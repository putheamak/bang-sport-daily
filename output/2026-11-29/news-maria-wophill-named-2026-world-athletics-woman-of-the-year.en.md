# Maria Wophill Named 2026 World Athletics Woman of the Year

*The Nigerian administrator is honoured for decades of leadership, board governance reforms, and youth track development across Africa.*

**News** · 2026-11-29

World Athletics named veteran Nigerian sports administrator Maria Wophill as the recipient of the 2026 Woman of the Year Award on Saturday, November 28, 2026. The international governing body announced the honour from its headquarters as part of its annual awards roll, citing Wophill’s decades of governance work, policy advocacy for gender equality, and youth development across Africa.

## Decades of governance in Nigeria

Wophill served as Secretary General of the Athletics Federation of Nigeria (AFN) from 2005 to 2013, directing national team administration and international championship preparations. During her eight years at the federation and across subsequent senior administrative posts inside Nigeria’s Federal Ministry of Youth and Sports Development, she campaigned for institutional governance reforms. Her advocacy helped secure formal approval for reserved leadership slots for women on executive boards across Nigeria’s national sports federations.

Her administrative work extended into global governance when she served an eight-year term on the World Athletics Women’s Committee between 2010 and 2018. In 2024, Wophill spearheaded the establishment of the Confederation of African Athletics (CAA) Gender Leadership Task Force, a continental body that coordinates leadership seminars and administrative training for women in athletics federations throughout Africa.

## Expanding continental and grassroots access

Beyond executive boardrooms, Wophill focused on coaching education and school-age sports development. She mentored emerging female coaches and sports administrators through the Women’s Sport Leadership Academy (WISH) programme, providing pathway training in sports management and technical officiating.

At the community level in Nigeria, Wophill founded and coordinated the Abuja Federal Capital Territory (FCT) Girls Sprints and Relays. The competition targets female students in junior and senior secondary schools, establishing a structured platform for sprint and relay racing while identifying young athletes for regional development programmes.

## Global recognition ahead of main awards

“This has been a truly humbling year and I am very proud to be acknowledged for the work I have done,” Wophill said in an official statement released by World Athletics. “I wish to express my sincere appreciation, especially to the leadership of World Athletics who have placed a great focus on integrity and consistent dedication to the development of athletics. This award has energised me to keep giving my best in all positions in athletics, with particular focus on gender leadership, safeguarding young athletes and mentoring within the sport.”

World Athletics President Sebastian Coe praised Wophill's career, pointing to her contributions to female representation and athlete safeguarding. The Woman of the Year announcement opened the presentation of the 2026 World Athletics Awards, with the federation scheduled to announce the male and female World Athletes of the Year, the Rising Stars, and the Moment of the Year on Sunday, November 29, 2026.
