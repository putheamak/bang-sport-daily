# Spurs Overcome 20-Point Deficit to Beat Lakers 99-96 in NBA Cup

*Stephon Castle scores 26 points and Victor Wembanyama grabs 16 rebounds as San Antonio secures a quarterfinal berth.*

**News** · 2026-11-29

On Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, the San Antonio Spurs overcame a 20-point first-half deficit to defeat the Los Angeles Lakers 99-96 at the Frost Bank Center, clinching a wild-card berth in the NBA Cup knockout rounds and eliminating Los Angeles from the in-season competition.

Stephon Castle scored a team-high 26 points on 10-of-15 shooting from the floor, while Victor Wembanyama added 25 points and 16 rebounds to lift San Antonio to a 16-4 overall season record. Los Angeles surged to an early 34-17 advantage in the second quarter and went into halftime ahead 54-38. San Antonio erased that gap with a 30-14 third quarter, limiting the Lakers to 6-of-24 shooting from the field and 2-of-13 from three-point territory. Keldon Johnson drained a three-pointer with two seconds remaining in the period to tie the score at 68-68 entering the fourth.

## Castle and Wembanyama execute in the clutch

San Antonio maintained momentum in the fourth quarter behind Castle, who sank a stepback three-pointer off an offensive rebound by De'Aaron Fox with 43.4 seconds left to establish a 96-90 lead. Austin Reaves answered for Los Angeles with a 25-foot three-pointer with 34 seconds remaining to pull within 96-93, and Jake LaRavia added two free throws with 7.7 seconds left to cut the margin to 96-95.

Fox, returning after missing two games with an injury, made a pair of free throws with 6.6 seconds remaining to push the lead to 98-95. Following an intentional foul, Los Angeles guard Quentin Grimes converted one of two free throws before Wembanyama secured the defensive rebound and split two free throws with 2.3 seconds left. Dylan Harper scored 14 points off the bench for the Spurs after missing four games with a hip ailment, while Fox contributed 12 points.

## Dončić struggles from deep as Lakers exit tournament

Luka Dončić led all scorers with 31 points, eight assists and six rebounds for the Lakers, but shot 1-of-13 from three-point range on 14-of-31 shooting overall. Reaves finished with 21 points on 5-of-9 three-point attempts in his return from a five-game absence caused by a left heel bruise, and center Walker Kessler grabbed 13 rebounds alongside 10 points.

Lakers head coach JJ Redick took responsibility for the fourth-quarter collapse. "I can think of five or six dumb things that I did tonight," Redick told reporters after the game. "But to beat these teams, you can't have those momentary mental lapses."

San Antonio finished West Group C play with a 3-1 record, advancing to the quarterfinals on point differential as the Western Conference wild card to face the Dallas Mavericks. The Spurs host the Boston Celtics on Sunday, Nov. 29, while the Lakers (15-4 overall, 2-2 in Cup play) are scheduled to play the Oklahoma City Thunder on Dec. 6.
