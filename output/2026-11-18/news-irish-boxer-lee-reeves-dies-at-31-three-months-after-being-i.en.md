# Irish Boxer Lee Reeves Dies at 31, Three Months After Being Injured in a Fight in Dublin

*Reeves was knocked out by Gary Cully in August and spent three months in a medically induced coma. His promoter, Queensberry, called him a role model and an inspiration to his community.*

**News** · 2026-11-18

## What happened

Irish boxer Lee Reeves has died at the age of 31, three months after he was seriously injured in a fight in Dublin. Reeves died on Monday, 16 November 2026, The Ring reported.

On 1 August, Reeves lost to Gary Cully in a junior welterweight bout at the 3Arena in Dublin. He was knocked down twice in the 10th round, lost consciousness and was carried from the ring on a stretcher, according to The Ring. He had emergency surgery for a brain injury and was placed in a medically induced coma, ESPN reported.

Last month he was moved to University Hospital Limerick, in his home city. He developed an infection over the weekend before his death, Sky Sports and The Ring reported. Family and friends had been hopeful about his recovery.

## Tributes

His promoter, Queensberry, confirmed his death. "Lee was an immensely talented boxer, as well as a true role model and inspiration to his community," the company said. Queensberry founder Frank Warren called the news heartbreaking, ESPN reported.

Cully paid tribute on Instagram, describing Reeves as "a character and a gent", and said there had never been any ill feeling between them. Reeves' former coach Jonathan O'Brien and his sister Jessie also posted messages, Sky Sports reported.

## His career

Reeves turned professional in November 2018 and won 16 of his 18 professional fights, 11 of them by knockout. Some of those fights took place in Canada and Colombia. Before turning professional he was a five-time national amateur champion, The Ring reported.

Away from the ring, he served as a soldier in the Irish Army's 12th Battalion, based in Limerick, and spoke publicly about mental health awareness. In 2023 he took part in the BBC reality series Survivor.
