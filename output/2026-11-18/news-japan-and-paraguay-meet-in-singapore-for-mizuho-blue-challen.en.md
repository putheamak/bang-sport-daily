# Japan and Paraguay Meet in Singapore for Mizuho Blue Challenge

*Travelling supporters gather at the National Stadium for the Kallang Football Series international fixture on November 18, 2026.*

**News** · 2026-11-18

## International Football Arrives in Singapore

Football fans from across the region have travelled to Singapore for the Mizuho Blue Challenge, an international fixture featuring the Japan men's national football team. The match takes place on November 18, 2026, at the National Stadium as part of the Kallang Football Series. 

Organised under the banner of the Mizuho Blue Challenge, the series brings high-profile international football directly to Southeast Asian sports fans outside of standard continental qualification windows. Travelling supporters have gathered in the city-state to watch the cross-confederation fixture unfold.

## Facing a Resolute Defence

Ahead of the fixture, scouting reports published by the Japan Football Association highlighted the specific tactical challenge posed by Paraguay. Analysis from the governing body pointed to Paraguay's resolute defence as a primary obstacle for the Japanese attack to break down at the venue.

Japan enters the match following an active international calendar, while the South American side brings its characteristic defensive organization to test the Asian powerhouse on neutral soil. Coaching staff from both federations have utilized the friendly window to evaluate squad depth and test combinations away from official qualification pressures.

## Broadening the Global Calendar

Staging the fixture in Singapore reflects a growing trend of major national teams taking matches to neutral venues in Southeast Asia to engage international fanbases. The Kallang Football Series provides local spectators with direct access to elite players who typically compete in domestic European leagues and major continental tournaments.

Following the final whistle at the National Stadium, both national squads will disperse back to their respective domestic and continental clubs ahead of upcoming league fixtures and upcoming tournament qualification rounds in 2027.
