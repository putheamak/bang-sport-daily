# China Win Four Golds at Beijing Speed Skating World Cup as Ning, Lian and Gao Deliver

*Ning Zhongyan won the 1,000m in a track record and the 1,500m, while Lian Ziwen and Gao Tingyu took one 500m race each at the Ice Ribbon.*

**News** · 2026-11-15

## What happened

China's speed skaters won four gold medals in the men's sprint and middle-distance races at the ISU Speed Skating World Cup in Beijing, held at the National Speed Skating Oval from 13 to 15 November 2026. It was the first World Cup meeting of the new season.

Ning Zhongyan won two of them. On the opening day he took the men's 1,000 metres in 1 minute 7.43 seconds, a track record for the oval known as the Ice Ribbon. On Saturday he won the 1,500 metres in 1:43.64, China News Service reported.

The other two golds came in the men's 500 metres, which was raced twice. Lian Ziwen won the first race on Saturday in 34.64 seconds, his first individual World Cup victory. Stefan Westenbroek of the Netherlands was second in 34.71 and Gao Tingyu, the 2022 Olympic champion at the distance, third in 34.80.

On Sunday the order changed. Gao won the second race in 34.61 seconds and Lian was second in 34.77, according to Sina. The two raced each other in the final pair.

## Other results

Lian also took bronze in the 1,000 metres on the opening day. In the women's 1,500 metres on Saturday, Han Mei finished third behind Ragne Wiklund of Norway and Joy Beune of the Netherlands. Dutch skaters filled all three podium places in the first women's 500 metres, where China's Tian Ruining was fifth.

## Key facts and numbers

- **Men's 1,000m (13 Nov):** Ning Zhongyan 1:07.43, track record; Lian Ziwen third.
- **Men's 1,500m (14 Nov):** Ning Zhongyan 1:43.64.
- **Men's 500m, race 1 (14 Nov):** Lian Ziwen 34.64, Stefan Westenbroek 34.71, Gao Tingyu 34.80.
- **Men's 500m, race 2 (15 Nov):** Gao Tingyu 34.61, Lian Ziwen 34.77.
- **Women's 1,500m (14 Nov):** Han Mei third.
- **China's medals in these races:** four gold, one silver, three bronze.

The final day also included the team pursuit and mass start races, which are not covered in this report.
