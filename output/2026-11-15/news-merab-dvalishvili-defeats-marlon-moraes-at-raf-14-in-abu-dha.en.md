# Merab Dvalishvili defeats Marlon Moraes at RAF 14 in Abu Dhabi

*The former UFC bantamweight champion secured a victory by fall in a freestyle wrestling match against the Brazilian veteran.*

**News** · 2026-11-15

## A Clash in Abu Dhabi

Former UFC bantamweight champion Merab Dvalishvili secured a victory by fall against former UFC fighter Marlon Moraes in a freestyle wrestling match. The encounter took place on 14 November 2026 at the RAF 14 tournament in Abu Dhabi, bringing two notable MMA veterans onto the wrestling mats.

Dvalishvili entered the fixture carrying the reputation of a dominant grappler within the Ultimate Fighting Championship's 61-kilogram division. His opponent, Moraes, previously challenged for the UFC bantamweight title and brought extensive mixed martial arts experience to the specialized grappling ruleset in the United Arab Emirates.

## Background of the Competitors

Both athletes built significant careers inside the world's premier mixed martial arts promotion before stepping into the RAF 14 tournament. Dvalishvili's background in wrestling has consistently defined his competitive approach, allowing him to control opponents through relentless pacing and takedowns. Moraes, known historically for his striking prowess and explosive power during his peak bantamweight run, tested his grappling defense against one of the sport's most active wrestlers.

Organized under the RAF banner, the fixture provided a platform for established fighters to compete under specific martial arts disciplines outside of their standard mixed martial arts schedules. The result adds another grappling victory to Dvalishvili's resume while giving Abu Dhabi combat sports fans a high-profile stylistic matchup between two veterans.

## Looking Ahead

Events like RAF 14 continue to attract prominent MMA names seeking alternative competition formats. For Dvalishvili, the victory in Abu Dhabi demonstrates his ongoing commitment to testing his core discipline on the mats. Meanwhile, Moraes adds another chapter to his long career in international combat sports as the organization looks toward future cards later in the calendar year.
