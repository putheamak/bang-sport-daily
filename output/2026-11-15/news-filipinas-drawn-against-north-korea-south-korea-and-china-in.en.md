# Filipinas Drawn Against North Korea, South Korea and China in Olympic Qualifiers

*The Philippine women's national football team faces a rigorous six-match campaign in Group B for a place at the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.*

**News** · 2026-11-15

The Philippine women's national football team has learned its path to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics after being placed in Group B of the Asian qualifiers. Revealed by the Philippine Football Federation, the draw sets up six fixtures against three of the most formidable programs in the Asian Football Confederation: North Korea, South Korea, and China.

The grouping pairs the squad with traditional powerhouses of the continental game. North Korea, South Korea, and China boast decades of international experience, multiple World Cup appearances, and consistent high rankings within the region. For the Philippines, the round-robin schedule within Group B represents an exacting test of depth and tactical organisation as the team targets a historic Olympic appearance in the United States.

## The Scale of the Group B Test

Navigating Group B requires contesting home-and-away style fixtures against opponents who have long shaped women's football in Asia. China remains a multi-time Asian champion and a global competitor, while both North Korea and South Korea possess rigorous youth structures and professional leagues that consistently feed talent into their senior national setups. The six matches will demand sustained physical and mental resilience from the Philippine squad across the qualification window.

## Dual Horizons for Philippine Football

The Olympic qualification campaign unfolds alongside broader competitive targets mapped out by the Philippine Football Federation. Alongside the pursuit of a berth at the 2028 Los Angeles Games, the national programme continues preparations for upcoming major international tournaments. These fixtures serve as both a measure of the team's rapid development over recent years and a definitive step toward establishing the country as a competitive force in elite Asian football.
