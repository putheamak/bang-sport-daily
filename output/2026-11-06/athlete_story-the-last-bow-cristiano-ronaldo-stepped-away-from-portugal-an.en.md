# The Last Bow: Cristiano Ronaldo Stepped Away From Portugal, and an Era Closed

*From a boy in Madeira to a global icon, Ronaldo's international journey has ended. Here is what it meant to those of us watching from afar.*

**Athlete story** · 2026-11-06

Here in Phnom Penh, the night air is warm and heavy, the kind of night where you stay up late just to watch the game. For over two decades, millions of us across Asia and the world shared a common rhythm: we woke up early, or stayed up late, to watch Cristiano Ronaldo pull on the red shirt of Portugal. 

Now, that chapter is finished. Ronaldo has announced his retirement from international football, bringing down the curtain on a staggering 23-year career with the national team. While he will continue to play his club football for Al-Nassr, the international stage will no longer feature the man who defined a generation of the global game.

## A Journey That Spanned Decades

The details of his international career read like a map of modern football history. He made his debut for Portugal back in 2003 as an eighteen-year-old winger with dyed tips in his hair and endless tricks at his feet. Over the years, that raw speed transformed into ruthless efficiency. He carried his nation through European Championships and World Cups, culminating in that glorious, emotional night in Paris in 2016 when Portugal lifted the Euro trophy.

Numbers alone struggle to capture what he achieved. While various records debate his exact appearance totals—with reports citing anywhere from 234 to 243 matches for his country—his status as the ultimate competitor is beyond dispute. He scored goals in quantities that defied logic, breaking international scoring records and dragging teams forward sheerly through force of will.

## What He Meant to Us

For young people in developing countries, especially across Asia where European football is beamed into our living rooms every weekend, Ronaldo was more than just an athlete. He was a masterclass in what is possible when relentless work meets supreme talent. Long after his teammates left the training pitch, Ronaldo stayed behind to practice his free kicks. 

I often think about what sport teaches our children. It is not just about the trophies; it is about the refusal to accept defeat, the discipline to wake up early, and the courage to carry the hopes of an entire nation on your shoulders. Ronaldo did that for Portugal through joy and heartbreak, through tears in finals and triumphs under the lights.

## The End of an Era

Every great story must eventually reach its final page. Seeing Ronaldo step away from international duty feels strange, like a constant landmark in the football landscape has finally shifted. Generations of children grew up knowing only a world where Ronaldo led the Portuguese attack.

As he focuses the remainder of his playing days on club football in Saudi Arabia with Al-Nassr, we are left to look back and appreciate the sheer longevity of his career. He showed us that greatness is not a fleeting moment, but a daily choice sustained over twenty-three years.

Thank you for the memories, Cristiano. The pitch feels a little different tonight.
