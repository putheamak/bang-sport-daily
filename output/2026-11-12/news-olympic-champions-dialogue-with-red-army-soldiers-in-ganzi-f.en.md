# Olympic champions dialogue with Red Army soldiers in Ganzi for Chinese Sports Spirit tour

*The 'Ode to Chinese Sports Spirit' campaign arrived in Sichuan's Ganzi Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture on November 11, 2026, linking modern athletic achievement with historical endurance near the Dadu River.*

**News** · 2026-11-12

Olympic champions gathered in Sichuan province on November 11, 2026, to engage in a symbolic dialogue with historical Red Army soldiers as part of the nationwide 'Ode to Chinese Sports Spirit' campaign. Hosted in Ganzi Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, the event brought together elite athletes, local youth, and sports administrators near the historic Dadu River.

## Bridging History and Sport Along the Dadu River

The programme, reported by China News Service, placed contemporary athletic achievement against the backdrop of the Long March. Organizers designed the Ganzi stop to connect the physical endurance required by competitive athletes with the historical sacrifices commemorated in the region. Participants discussed how the perseverance demonstrated on Olympic podiums echoes the resilience of past generations who traversed the challenging terrain of western China.

The Dadu River carries profound historical weight in Chinese history due to the Ganzi region's central role during the Long March. By placing Olympic medalists in dialogue with the memory of Red Army soldiers, the Ministry of Sport and regional organizers sought to frame competitive sporting grit as a direct continuation of historical national perseverance. Similar stops on the nationwide tour have consistently paired modern training discipline with local revolutionary history.

## Youth Engagement and Regional Development

Beyond the main commemorative forum, visiting athletes interacted with student-athletes and local schools across the prefecture. The initiative forms part of a broader push by sports authorities to promote physical education and cultural values in remote and ethnic minority regions of western China. Local education bureaus facilitated workshops where young participants learned about training methodologies and goal-setting directly from national team representatives.

The event leaves the Ganzi prefecture as one of the key regional stops for the ongoing sports spirit tour, which travels across Chinese provinces to document the intersection of athletic excellence and national heritage. Future legs of the campaign are scheduled to visit additional historical landmarks across other provinces to expand youth sports outreach in underserved areas.
