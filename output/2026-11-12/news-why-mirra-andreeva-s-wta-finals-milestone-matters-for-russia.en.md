# Why Mirra Andreeva's WTA Finals Milestone Matters for Russian Tennis

*An explainer on the numbers and history behind Andreeva reaching the semi-finals at the season-ending championship.*

**News** · 2026-11-12

## What happened

At the WTA Finals, Mirra Andreeva advanced from her round-robin group in the singles tournament, securing a place in the semi-finals. Major sports outlets including Sport-Экспресс, Championat, and Sportbox reported on the milestone achievement following the conclusion of the group matches.

## Why it is being talked about

Andreeva's progression marks the first time a Russian singles player has reached this stage of the season-ending championship in a decade. Coverage from Championat noted that she secured a spot in the tournament's semi-finals, ending a ten-year drought for Russian tennis at the elite eight-player event. The achievement places her alongside the historic performances of previous Russian competitors who last contested the tournament's latter stages in 2016.

## Key facts and numbers

Reporting from Sport-Экспресс and Championat confirmed the ten-year timeline, establishing 2016 as the last year a Russian singles player advanced past the group stage at the WTA Finals. Sportbox detailed her performance within the tournament group, highlighting a notable victory against Aryna Sabalenka that contributed to propelling her forward in the competition.

## What happens next

With the group stage concluded, Andreeva moves forward into the semi-finals of the WTA Finals to face the remaining contenders in the year-end bracket for a chance to play in the championship match.
