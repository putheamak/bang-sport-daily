# Cody Garbrandt Confirms UFC Retirement Fight Set for Cleveland in February

*The former bantamweight champion reveals plans to finish his UFC contract on home soil after a 12-year octagon career.*

**News** · 2026-11-28

Former UFC bantamweight champion Cody Garbrandt announced on Friday, November 27, 2026, that his upcoming bout at UFC Cleveland on February 13, 2027, will serve as the final fight of his mixed martial arts career in the promotion.

Speaking to MMA Fighting and MMA Junkie in Chicago ahead of his wrestling match against Sergio Pettis at RAF 15, the 35-year-old Ohio native confirmed he is in discussions with UFC matchmaker Sean Shelby to fulfill the last remaining fight on his contract in his home state.

## Final walk on home soil

"In my head, my mind, my heart, I know that it will be my last one," Garbrandt said when discussing his planned appearance at Rocket Mortgage FieldHouse. While the UFC has not yet confirmed an opponent, the Cleveland card is slated to feature a five-round middleweight headliner between Bo Nickal and Joe Pyfer.

Garbrandt enters the scheduled farewell bout with a professional record of 15 wins and 8 losses. When asked about potential opponents, he named former two-time champion and former Team Alpha Male teammate T.J. Dillashaw as his preferred fantasy matchup, despite Dillashaw having retired in 2022.

## From Cruz masterclass to octagon decline

Garbrandt reached the pinnacle of his MMA career on December 30, 2016, at UFC 207 in Las Vegas, where he handed Dominick Cruz a unanimous decision defeat to capture the bantamweight championship and advance to 11-0. That performance stood out as one of the sharpest boxing displays in division history.

His championship reign lasted 11 months. Dillashaw stopped Garbrandt by second-round knockout at UFC 217 at Madison Square Garden in November 2017, then secured another first-round stoppage in their rematch at UFC 227 in Los Angeles in August 2018. Following those defeats, Garbrandt posted a 4-8 record over his subsequent 12 UFC appearances.

## One contract fight remaining

Garbrandt’s recent form includes three losses in his past four octagon outings. He fell to Deiveson Figueiredo by submission at UFC 300 in April 2024 and dropped a unanimous decision against Raoni Barcelos in June 2025. 

Although he secured a unanimous decision victory over Xiao Long at UFC 326 in March 2026, he was stopped by first-round knockout against Adrian Yanez at UFC 329 on July 11, 2026. Garbrandt briefly removed his gloves inside the octagon following the Yanez loss before confirming on social media that he planned one final walk. His wrestling exhibition against Pettis on Saturday, November 28, 2026, at the NOW Arena in Hoffman Estates, Illinois, marks his final competitive tuneup before closing out his UFC tenure.
