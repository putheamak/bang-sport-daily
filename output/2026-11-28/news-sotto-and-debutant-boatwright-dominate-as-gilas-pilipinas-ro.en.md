# Sotto and Debutant Boatwright Dominate as Gilas Pilipinas Rout Syria 97-58

*A 39-point victory in Malaysia lifts the Philippines to 5-4 in FIBA World Cup qualifying ahead of a pivotal clash with Iran.*

**News** · 2026-11-28

Gilas Pilipinas recorded a 97-58 victory over Syria on Friday, 27 November 2026, at the Karisma Arena in Seremban, Malaysia, to open the fifth window of the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 Asian Qualifiers. The 39-point margin gave the Philippines their third consecutive win in Group E and lifted their overall qualifying record to 5-4.

Naturalized power forward Bennie Boatwright collected 16 points and 13 rebounds in his national team debut, partnering center Kai Sotto in the frontcourt. Sotto posted 18 points and 10 rebounds in 17 minutes and 40 seconds on the court. Head coach Tim Cone commended Boatwright's fast adjustment, stating, "We only had a few practices with Bennie Boatwright, and yet, he's fitting into us like a glove. He didn't go out and force anything, and stayed in what we do".

## Record shooting breaks the deadlock

After an 18-18 tie in the opening quarter, the Philippines built a 43-32 advantage by halftime behind a 21-6 scoring run. Kevin Quiambao scored 18 points off the bench, converting six three-pointers on eight attempts to set a single-game record for a Philippine player in the qualifiers. Gilas outscored Syria 29-13 in the third quarter and 25-13 in the fourth, extending the advantage to a peak of 40 points at 95-55. Juan Gomez de Liaño added 11 points, while CJ Perez and Mike Phillips contributed 10 points apiece.

## Glass control and defensive clamps

The Philippines controlled the interior, finishing with a 60-30 rebounding advantage and outscoring Syria 42-26 inside the paint. Gilas made 14 of 28 attempts from beyond the arc (50 percent) while limiting Syria to 30.6 percent shooting from the floor. Syrian naturalized guard D'Angelo Harrison scored 10 points on 3-of-15 shooting, while Mohamad Bilal Atli led the Syrian offense with 12 points and nine rebounds. The loss dropped Syria to 2-7, eliminating them from World Cup contention.

## Group E standings tighten

The victory pulled the Philippines into a three-way tie for third place in Group E alongside Jordan and Iran at 5-4. Earlier on Friday, undefeated Australia clinched their spot at the 2027 World Cup at 9-0 by beating Iran 84-67, while New Zealand defeated Jordan 103-64 to improve to 7-2. Gilas face Iran on Sunday, 29 November, at the Karisma Arena before concluding the qualifying tournament on home soil in Cebu City in February 2027.
