# Diana Shnaider Overwhelms Kamilla Rakhimova 13-5 at Northern Palmyra Trophies

*The world No. 11 won 11 of the last 12 games to extend team Sphinxes' lead at KSK Arena.*

**News** · 2026-11-28

Diana Shnaider defeated Kamilla Rakhimova 13-5 by total games at the Northern Palmyra Trophies exhibition tournament in St. Petersburg on Saturday, November 28, 2026. Playing on the hard courts of KSK Arena, the Russian world No. 11 dropped the opening frame before sweeping the remaining two periods to take the match 2-4, 6-0, 5-1. The result handed the Sphinxes an aggregate 39-34 advantage over the Lions in the overall team standings.

## Reversing the Deficit in Timed Sets

Rakhimova, who began representing Uzbekistan on the international circuit and holds a career-high ranking of world No. 44, seized early control by winning the opening 20-minute set 4-2. The tournament uses an experimental format where matches are limited to three 20-minute sessions—with a total match cap of 75 minutes—and every individual game won counts toward the team scoreboard.

Shnaider adjusted quickly after the restart. Dictating baseline rallies, the 22-year-old shut out Rakhimova 6-0 in the middle frame and maintained the pressure to close out the third set 5-1. Over the final 40 minutes of action, Shnaider outscored Rakhimova 11-1.

## Team Momentum and Friday's Precedent

Shnaider is competing for the Sphinxes under Serbian captain Janko Tipsarevic, the former ATP world No. 8. The Lions are captained by Elena Vesnina, the 2016 Olympic doubles gold medalist. 

The win marked Shnaider's second high-profile outing of the weekend. On Friday, November 27, she opened the tournament by defeating recently retired British player Dan Evans 5-2 in a singles "Battle of the Sexes" exhibition under modified court rules. The Sphinxes built further on Shnaider's Saturday triumph when teammate Karen Khachanov beat France's Corentin Moutet 12-10 (2-5, 6-1, 4-4), pushing their lead over the Lions to 51-44.

## Context of the St. Petersburg Exhibition

Organized outside the official WTA and ATP calendars, the Northern Palmyra Trophies event has run annually since 2022, when professional tennis tours suspended official Russian tournaments. Primary sponsorship comes from state energy corporation Gazprom. While awarding no ranking points, the tournament attracted active tour players including Alexander Bublik, Yulia Putintseva, Anastasia Potapova, and Adrian Mannarino. The participation of international competitors drew public criticism from Ukrainian Tennis Federation executive director Evgeniy Zukin, who warned of reputational consequences.

For Shnaider, the event serves as an off-season exhibition following a career-best campaign. Her 2026 season included a run to the French Open semi-finals and a title at the WTA 500 in Tokyo on November 1. Competition at KSK Arena concludes on Sunday, November 29.
