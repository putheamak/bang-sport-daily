# Henan Sports Authority Refutes 200,000-Yuan Basketball Certificate Scam

*Officials warn parents after brokers promised guaranteed tournament slots and manipulated game data for university admission credentials*

**News** · 2026-11-28

On Saturday, November 28, 2026, the Henan Provincial Ball Games Management Center issued an official statement dismissing underground claims that intermediaries could secure a Chinese National Second-Class Athlete basketball certificate for 200,000 yuan ($28,000) as a complete fabrication.

The clarification followed media reports revealing that unlicensed brokers had approached families of school-age athletes, offering paid routes through the provincial tournament structure. The middlemen claimed to hold partnerships across Henan, Liaoning, and Jiangxi provinces, promising guaranteed roster spots for the upcoming Henan Provincial Basketball Championship in early 2027. The package included fabricated game minutes, tampered data under the official K8 digital basketball tracking system, and expedited transfers of academic and household registrations.

## Closed-Loop Oversight and Fee Warnings

As the governing body responsible for staging Henan's provincial basketball tournaments, the Henan Provincial Ball Games Management Center stated that every stage of registration and athlete evaluation is strictly insulated from outside interference.

"The qualification of participants and the accreditation of athlete ranks in our province's basketball events are subject to closed-loop supervision throughout the entire process," the center stated in its official notice. "There is no channel to buy entry spots or certificates."

The center stressed that official athlete qualification procedures carry no fee whatsoever. Officials instructed parents and players not to transfer money to third parties and urged anyone approached by illicit brokers to notify public security authorities immediately.

## The High Stakes of Sports Credentials in China

In China's education framework, the National Second-Class Athlete (*Erji*) status issued under the standards of the General Administration of Sport of China plays a decisive role in higher education admissions. Student-athletes holding the credential qualify for high-level athletic squad programs and national sports entrance examinations, which allow universities to grant admission with significantly lower academic cut-offs on the Gaokao. This demand has driven underground brokers to target provincial tournaments, where placement criteria can grant athletes the rank.

Established in December 2002 under the Henan Provincial Sports Bureau, the Ball Games Management Center administers 12 sports squads across basketball, football, volleyball, baseball, and softball. Provincial sports federations have rolled out automated stat tracking, including the K8 platform, to prevent roster padding and ghost appearances, making third-party tampering claims a priority target for sports integrity regulators.
