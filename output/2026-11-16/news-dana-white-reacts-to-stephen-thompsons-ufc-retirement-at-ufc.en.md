# Dana White Reacts to Stephen Thompson’s UFC Retirement at UFC 334

*UFC CEO Dana White addresses Stephen 'Wonderboy' Thompson's retirement and offers a potential new role following a memorable night of fights.*

**News** · 2026-11-16

## What happened
Following UFC 334 on November 16, 2026, UFC CEO Dana White reacted publicly to Stephen "Wonderboy" Thompson's retirement from active mixed martial arts competition. During the post-event media conference, White addressed Thompson's career conclusion and offered the veteran fighter the chance to test for a new job within the promotion. Outlets including Yahoo Sports, MMA Fighting, and Bloody Elbow reported on the retirement developments.

## Why it is being talked about
Thompson's exit generated widespread discussion across the combat sports community, with several reports labeling the moment a storybook ending. The emotional conclusion of his career left UFC commentator Joe Rogan moved to tears during the broadcast, with Rogan remarking that he had never seen that happen in his life. Beyond Thompson's farewell, UFC 334 drew considerable attention for its heavyweight main event featuring Gane versus Hokit, which shifted White's perspective on Josh Hokit. White also revealed his scoring of the main event and addressed media queries.

## Key facts and numbers
Eight independent media outlets covered the fallout from UFC 334 on November 16, 2026. The post-fight press conference also produced viral media moments, including a sharp exchange when Dana White reacted with confusion to a long-winded question from a reporter, asking what had just been asked, as documented by Fox News. Sports Illustrated highlighted three notable nuggets dropped by White during the session regarding the Gane and Hokit card.

## What happens next
Stephen Thompson steps away from active bouts to conclude his professional fighting career, while the possibility remains open for an administrative or organizational role evaluated by Dana White. The wider division and roster developments from UFC 334 continue to shape the promotion's upcoming schedule.
