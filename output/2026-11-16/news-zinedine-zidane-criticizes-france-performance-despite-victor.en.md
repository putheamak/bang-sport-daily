# Zinedine Zidane Criticizes France Performance Despite Victory Over Turkey

*France head coach expresses dissatisfaction with team display while praising captain Kylian Mbappé in the UEFA Nations League.*

**News** · 2026-11-16

## What happened
France national team head coach Zinedine Zidane publicly voiced his frustration following his side's fixture against Turkey on Sunday, 16 November 2026. Reporting from major outlets including Sport-Express and Sport Mail confirmed that the French manager expressed direct displeasure with the team's overall execution on the pitch. Despite securing a victory in the UEFA Nations League encounter, Zidane stated that his feelings of disappointment were fully shared by the players themselves.

## Why it is being talked about
The post-match remarks generated substantial media coverage due to the stark contrast between Zidane's critique of the collective display and his glowing assessment of team captain Kylian Mbappé. According to Sport Mail, Zidane defended his forward's individual output, asserting that Mbappé deserved to score two or three goals during the match. The France manager emphasized that the captain had successfully created his own scoring opportunities throughout the fixture and praised his leadership execution under difficult match conditions.

## Key facts and numbers
The match formed part of the ongoing UEFA Nations League tournament schedule, which has drawn extensive analysis across European sports media platforms such as Sport24.ru and Sport-Express. Zidane specifically highlighted the immense workload shouldered by Mbappé, noting that the captain handled his responsibilities ideally despite the broader tactical shortcomings displayed by the rest of the French squad.

## What happens next
Following the conclusion of this UEFA Nations League fixture, both the French national team and Turkey continue their respective tournament preparations. National federations look ahead to upcoming international windows, where squad depth and tactical adjustments will face further evaluation as qualification stages and continental competitions progress.
