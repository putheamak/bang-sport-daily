# Russian Tennis Player Anna Kalinskaya Announces Engagement to Tomas Ferrari

*The world number 26 shared the news on social media alongside Argentinian financier Tomas Ferrari.*

**News** · 2026-11-16

## A New Chapter for the WTA Star

Russian tennis player Anna Kalinskaya announced her engagement to Argentinian businessman and financier Tomas Ferrari on Saturday, November 15, 2026. The 27-year-old athlete shared the personal news with her followers on Instagram, posting a joint photograph accompanied by an engagement ring emoji and the caption, "I can't wait for our next chapter."

Media reports indicate that Kalinskaya and Ferrari previously dated in 2021 before parting ways. The couple renewed their relationship in 2025, following the conclusion of Kalinskaya's high-profile relationship with Italian tennis player and former world number one Jannik Sinner. In recent months, Ferrari has been a frequent presence at international tennis tournaments, regularly supporting Kalinskaya from the stadium stands and players' boxes during her tour matches.

## Professional Standing and Circuit Context

Kalinskaya currently occupies the 26th position in the Women's Tennis Association (WTA) singles rankings. The engagement announcement arrives during the professional tennis off-season, a period when athletes pause their demanding tournament schedules to rest and prepare for the upcoming year's circuit calendar.

Throughout her career on the international tour, Kalinskaya has maintained a consistent presence among the top tier of women's tennis, securing notable victories against elite opponents and navigating the rigorous travel demands of the global tennis circuit. The personal milestone comes as the Russian competitor looks ahead to her preparations for the next competitive season.
