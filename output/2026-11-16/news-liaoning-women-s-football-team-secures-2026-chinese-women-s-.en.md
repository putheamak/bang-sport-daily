# Liaoning Women's Football Team Secures 2026 Chinese Women's Super League Title

*A crucial draw against Beijing allows Liaoning to claim their maiden top-flight league crown with a match to spare.*

**News** · 2026-11-16

On Saturday, 15 November 2026, the Liaoning women's football team clinched the 2026 Chinese Women's Super League championship. Playing against Beijing Construction Engineering, the Liaoning side secured the required result to unseat their rivals with a match to spare in the domestic calendar, ending the penultimate round of the season at the summit of the standings.

## A Historic First Crown for Liaoning

The championship marks the first-ever Chinese Women's Super League title in the history of the Liaoning club. Beijing entered the fixture needing a victory to keep their championship hopes alive and claim the trophy for themselves, but the eventual draw allowed Liaoning to maintain an unassailable lead at the top of the table before the final round of matches.

Beijing Construction Engineering were left to reflect on missed opportunities in the title race, having watched the Liaoning squad celebrate the trophy presentation on the penultimate matchday of the season after failing to secure the win they required.

## League Implications and What Follows

With the title secured with a game remaining, Liaoning will finish their 2026 campaign at the top of the table as domestic champions. The final round of fixtures will serve to conclude the calendar, while Liaoning celebrates a breakthrough achievement in the history of the Chinese Women's Super League.
