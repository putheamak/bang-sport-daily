# China Drawn With North Korea and South Korea in 2028 Olympic Qualifiers

*The Asian Football Confederation reveals the group stage lineup for the road to the Los Angeles Olympic Games.*

**News** · 2026-11-13

China's women's national football team has landed in a demanding group alongside North Korea and South Korea for the Asian qualifiers of the 2028 Los Angeles Olympic Games. Outlets including Zigong News and China News Service reported the official draw results on November 12, 2026, setting up a high-stakes regional showdown for the continental tournament.

## A Heavyweight East Asian Group

The grouping brings together three of the most competitive programs in Asian women's football. Matches involving China, North Korea, and South Korea carry deep historical rivalries and intense tactical battles within regional competitions. The presence of both Korean national teams alongside China eliminates any margin for error during the opening phase of the qualification campaign.

## The Road to Los Angeles

Participants in this qualification cycle are competing for a direct path to the 2028 Olympic Games in Los Angeles, California. The Asian Football Confederation structures these qualifiers to test regional contenders through demanding group fixtures before advancing to decisive knockout rounds. Teams must navigate this initial triangular pressure cooker to keep their Olympic ambitions alive.

## Next Steps for the Squad

Further details regarding exact match schedules, specific host venues, and complete group rosters will be released by the Asian Football Confederation in the coming weeks. Coaching staffs from China, North Korea, and South Korea will use the intervening period to finalize preparations as the tournament kickoff approaches.
