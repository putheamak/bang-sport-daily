# 2026 China International Boxing Open Concludes in Zunyi

*International athletes and domestic competitors gather in Guizhou for multi-day tournament*

**News** · 2026-11-13

The 2026 China International Boxing Open concluded on Wednesday, November 11, in Zunyi, Guizhou Province. The multi-day competition brought together an international field of boxers to compete across various weight categories, marking a significant addition to the regional sports calendar in southwest China.

## Global Participation and Technical Standards

The tournament served as a platform for both established international talent and emerging domestic prospects. Among the notable participants was Olympic champion Alvarez Borges, who competed and participated in promotional activities throughout the event. Following his bouts, Borges remarked on the quality of the facilities and the organization, telling state media that Guizhou demonstrates an increasingly international vision for the sport of boxing. 

Organizers emphasized that the event was designed to meet international technical standards, providing a venue for athletes to gain ranking points and competitive experience. The presence of high-profile international figures like Borges is intended to elevate the profile of local training programs and encourage cross-border knowledge exchange between coaching staffs.

## Strategic Growth in Guizhou

Zunyi’s selection as the host city aligns with a broader national strategy to decentralize major sporting events. Historically, international-level combat sports fixtures in China were concentrated in coastal hubs such as Beijing, Shanghai, or Guangzhou. By hosting the Open in Zunyi, local officials aim to leverage the city’s developing sports infrastructure to foster a regional hub for boxing.

This event follows a series of recent investments in Guizhou’s sports facilities, aimed at hosting both national and international tournaments. For local athletes, the proximity of an international-standard competition reduces travel costs and provides exposure to diverse fighting styles that are typically unavailable outside of major international circuits. The tournament concluded with a series of final bouts that determined the podium positions across the weight classes, with local authorities confirming that the event successfully met its attendance and performance targets.

## Looking Ahead

The successful staging of the 2026 Open is expected to influence the scheduling of future combat sports events in the province. The Chinese Boxing Association has indicated that hosting international fixtures in inland provinces is a priority for the 2027 season, as the country continues to expand its boxing talent pool. Details regarding the next iteration of the tournament, including potential dates and expanded weight categories, are expected to be announced by the national governing body in early 2027.
