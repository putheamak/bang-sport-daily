# Speed Skating World Cup Returns to Beijing’s 'Ice Ribbon'

*Olympic gold medalists Ning Zhongyan and Gao Tingyu headline the ISU World Cup season opener as local technicians take charge of ice production.*

**News** · 2026-11-13

The National Speed Skating Oval in Beijing hosts the opening leg of the 2026-2027 International Skating Union (ISU) Speed Skating World Cup, with competition beginning on Thursday, November 13. The event marks the return of elite international speed skating to the venue famously dubbed the "Ice Ribbon," which served as the centerpiece for the 2022 Winter Olympic Games.

Leading the host nation's squad are Olympic gold medalist Gao Tingyu and world-class contender Ning Zhongyan. Both athletes enter this season—the first full cycle since the 2026 Milano Cortina preparations intensified—with a focus on recalibrating their form. According to reports from state media outlets including China News Service and The Beijing News, both skaters have emphasized a "relaxed" approach to this opening weekend, aiming to establish their racing rhythm rather than chasing immediate peak performances.

## Domestic Technical Milestone

Beyond the performances on the track, this year’s competition marks a significant operational shift for the venue. For the first time since its construction, the National Speed Skating Oval has utilized an entirely domestic ice-making team to prepare the rink. Organizers confirmed that the surface underwent specific upgrades to maintain the high-speed conditions that allowed for multiple Olympic records during the 2022 Games. The goal remains to preserve the venue's reputation as one of the fastest ice surfaces in the world while demonstrating the technical proficiency of China's independent facility management teams.

## Building Toward Future Milestones

For Ning Zhongyan, the Beijing leg serves as a critical opportunity to test recent training adjustments under competitive pressure. Ning, who has consistently ranked among the world’s top skaters in the 1000m and 1500m distances, noted in recent interviews that he is currently "finding his rhythm within a state of relaxation." Meanwhile, Gao Tingyu, the 500m champion from the 2022 Winter Olympics, continues his transition into the new quadrennial, with local fans showing high interest in his return to the track where he secured his historic gold medal.

The Beijing event is the first of several high-profile stops on the ISU World Cup calendar. Following the conclusion of the races on Sunday, the circuit will move to subsequent international venues as athletes continue to accrue points necessary for World Championship qualification. Public ticket sales for the Beijing event opened earlier this week, with organizers reporting sustained interest from local spectators eager to witness the return of international speed skating to the capital city.
