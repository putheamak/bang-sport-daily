# Melkadze and Sadulaev Strike to Put Akhmat Two Up at CSKA Moscow

*Second-half goals from Lechi Sadulaev and Georgi Melkadze put Rolan Gusev's side in control at the VEB Arena.*

**News** · 2026-11-22

Akhmat Grozny seized control against CSKA Moscow at the VEB Arena on Saturday, November 21, 2026, building a 2-0 advantage midway through the second half of their round 15 Russian Premier League fixture.

Following a goalless opening 45 minutes handled by referee Artem Chistyakov, winger Lechi Sadulaev opened the scoring from the penalty spot in the 52nd minute. Eleven minutes later, forward Georgi Melkadze doubled the visitors' lead in the 63rd minute, driving a first-time effort through the legs of CSKA goalkeeper Maksim Borisko after being played through by Angolan winger Egas Cacintura.

## Quickfire double stuns VEB Arena
The sudden two-goal deficit exposed defensive adjustments in Dmitry Igdisamov's CSKA lineup. The Moscow side entered the match without veteran goalkeeper Igor Akinfeev, while 22-year-old central defender Matvey Lukin was sidelined due to a training deficit following illness. That forced Dzhamalutdin Abdulkadyrov into the starting defense alongside Brazilian centre-back João Victor.

Akhmat took advantage through incisive transitions. Sadulaev's converted spot-kick marked his fourth league goal of the 2026/2027 campaign, while Melkadze's clinical finish rewarded a lively attacking unit that also included Kazakhstan international forward Maksim Samorodov, who started on the flank following the November international break.

## Gusev builds early momentum
The performance continued an immediate uptick in form for Akhmat under new head coach Rolan Gusev. The former CSKA midfielder was appointed during the autumn to replace Stanislav Cherchesov and secured a 1-0 home victory over Rodina Moscow in his debut on November 7. That result halted a frustrating seven-match winless run in all competitions.

At the VEB Arena, Gusev's tactical setup effectively stifled a CSKA side featuring leading scorer Ivan Oblyakov and Argentine striker Luciano Gondou. Although the hosts dominated possession with 69 percent before the interval, they struggled to break down Akhmat's low block and were repeatedly punished on counter-attacks.

## Relegation buffer and title race fallout
Before kickoff, CSKA occupied fifth place in the Premier League table with 26 points from 14 matches, sitting five points behind leaders Zenit St. Petersburg. Igdisamov's squad arrived seeking a rebound after conceding four goals in a 4-2 defeat away to FC Krasnodar on November 7 and drawing 0-0 against FC Orenburg a week earlier.

Akhmat entered the weekend in 12th position with 14 points, just three points above the direct relegation places. Establishing a two-goal cushion against a top-five opponent provides a vital morale boost ahead of their Russian Cup group encounter with Krasnodar on November 25. For CSKA, the setback deepens a difficult November stretch before hosting Zenit on November 28.
