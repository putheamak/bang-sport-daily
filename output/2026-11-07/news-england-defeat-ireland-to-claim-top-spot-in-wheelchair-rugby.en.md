# England defeat Ireland to claim top spot in Wheelchair Rugby League World Cup group

*A comprehensive 68-32 victory preserves a 100% group stage record and sets up a semi-final clash with co-hosts Australia.*

**News** · 2026-11-07

England confirmed their dominance in Pool A of the Wheelchair Rugby League World Cup by defeating Ireland 68-32. The result, finalized on Thursday, November 5, 2026, ensured that the squad finished the group stage with an unblemished record of three consecutive wins.

From the opening whistle, the English attack established a steady rhythm, accumulating points through structured passing and clinical conversion work. Ireland fought back with sustained pressure and periods of inventive play, crossing the try line multiple times, but the early deficit proved too difficult to overcome as the match progressed.

## Semi-Final Showdown Against Australia

Topping Pool A carries immediate high-stakes consequences for the knockout rounds. England advance directly to the semi-finals, where they will face tournament co-hosts Australia on Tuesday.

The fixture pits two of the sport's most competitive programs against each other with a spot in the world final on the line. Australia brings strong home support and tactical discipline into the match, while England carries the momentum of an unbeaten group campaign.

## Tournament Context and Progression

The expanded format of the current tournament highlights the global growth of wheelchair rugby league, drawing competitive matches across multiple venues. For England, maintaining defensive intensity will remain a priority following the 32 points conceded against Ireland, a total that coaching staff will analyze ahead of the Tuesday encounter.

As the tournament narrows to the final four teams, tactical execution and squad depth will dictate who advances. The winner of the upcoming semi-final will move one step closer to lifting the international trophy.
