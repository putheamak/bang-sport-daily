# Magomed Magomedov Edges Lucas Gabriel in ONE Debut to Extend Undefeated Streak

*The 31-year-old took a split decision at Lumpinee Stadium after surviving a dramatic final round to put himself in lightweight title contention.*

**News** · 2026-11-07

Bangkok — Magomed Magomedov maintained his unblemished professional record on Friday night, surviving a fierce late onslaught from Lucas Gabriel to earn a split-decision victory in his ONE Championship debut at Lumpinee Stadium.

The 31-year-old stepped into the main event of ONE Fight Night 49 on short notice to replace the injured Alibeg Rasulov. The victory improved Magomedov's career record to 16-0 and handed the 26-year-old Gabriel his second professional defeat.

From the opening bell, Magomedov looked to impose his grappling credentials. He quickly closed the distance, timed his entry, and brought Gabriel to the canvas, neutralising the Brazilian's striking with relentless positional control throughout the opening five minutes.

## Wrestling control staves off late rally

Magomedov pursued a similar tactical path in the second round, shooting early to bring the action back to the canvas. Gabriel adjusted well during the scrambles, successfully fending off sustained ground control and creating space on the feet.

Once upright, Gabriel landed a sharp head kick that forced Magomedov into another defensive takedown attempt, which the Brazilian managed to defend. That exchange hinted at shifting momentum, setting up a frantic third and final frame.

Magomedov initially reasserted authority in the decider, securing another takedown and smothering Gabriel on the mat. However, Gabriel scrambled effectively, reversed position in the closing minute, and unloaded a flurry of knees that visibly troubled Magomedov until the final bell.

Two of the three ringside judges scored the contest in Magomedov's favour, valuing his cumulative grappling dominance and control time over Gabriel's late surge.

## Title contention beckons in Bangkok

The short-notice victory positions Magomedov directly into the title conversation in ONE's lightweight MMA division. Gabriel had entered the cage boasting an 11-1 ledger and had been unbeaten in the promotion, making the newcomer's debut test particularly steep.

Magomedov's immediate future may now intersect with newly crowned ONE lightweight MMA world champion Tye Ruotolo. The American submission specialist captured the belt by defeating longtime champion Christian Lee at The Inner Circle 37 earlier on the same day.

With Rasulov sidelined by injury and Gabriel turned back, Magomedov's ability to grind out a victory under adverse preparation makes him a compelling fresh challenger for the division's new kingpin.
