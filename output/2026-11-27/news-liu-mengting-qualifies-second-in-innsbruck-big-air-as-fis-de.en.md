# Liu Mengting Qualifies Second in Innsbruck Big Air as FIS Debuts Two-Run Final Format

*China's freeski Olympian scored 164.50 at Bergisel, while a new zero-error finals rule reshapes the 2026–27 World Cup opener.*

**News** · 2026-11-27

China’s Liu Mengting secured second place in women’s freeski qualifying on Friday at the FIS Big Air World Cup in Innsbruck, Austria, posting 164.50 points at Bergisel Stadium to advance to the finals.

Following a four-hour weather delay caused by heavy snowfall across Tyrol, Switzerland’s Mathilde Gremaud led the field with 175.25 points, while Austria’s Lara Wolf qualified third. Liu, who finished seventh in Big Air at the Milano Cortina 2026 Olympic Winter Games with two 1440-degree rotations, anchored a nine-athlete Chinese contingent in Austria that includes freeskiers Han Linshan, 19-year-old debutant Zhang Jiaying, and men's competitor Lin Hao.

## Stricter Two-Run Scoring Tested at Bergisel

The Innsbruck stop, running from 26 to 28 November under the Airdrenaline banner, serves as the opening fixture of the 2026–27 FIS Park & Pipe World Cup calendar. It marks the first time Bergisel has hosted a FIS World Cup Big Air event, and Innsbruck’s first urban Big Air contest since 2013.

The International Ski and Snowboard Federation is using the Austrian stop to debut a revised finals format. Instead of the traditional three-run system where only a competitor's top two scores count, finalists receive exactly two attempts, and both scores are added together. The rule change eliminates the throwaway run, increasing the scoring penalty on falls and shifting emphasis toward execution consistency.

## Snowboard Roster Shifts Ahead of Beijing

In the snowboard Big Air division, Japan enters the competition led by Milano Cortina 2026 Olympic champions Kira Kimura and Kokomo Murase, while 35-year-old two-time Olympic champion Anna Gasser leads the Austrian hosts in her final World Cup season. 

China’s snowboard roster is led by Ge Chunyu and Zhang Xiaonan, joined by Liu Haoyu, Zhao Yingxu, and Jin Rongxi. Men's reigning Big Air Crystal Globe winner Su Yiming bypassed Innsbruck due to an injury, targeting his return for the second tour stop at Beijing’s Shougang Big Air from 3 to 5 December. Following Beijing, the four-stop Big Air tour travels to Kreischberg, Austria, and Tignes, France, in January 2027.

Simultaneously, China's domestic snow season begins on 28 and 29 November at the National Ski Jumping Centre in Chongli, where the "Snow Ruyi" ski resort hosts 100 athletes from 15 nations for the FIS Snowboard Alpine World Cup opener in parallel giant slalom and mixed team disciplines.
