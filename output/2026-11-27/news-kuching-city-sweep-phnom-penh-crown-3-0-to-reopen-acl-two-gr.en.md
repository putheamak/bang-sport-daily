# Kuching City Sweep Phnom Penh Crown 3-0 to Reopen ACL Two Group H

*Safawi Rasid, Jerome Mpacko, and Dylan Wenzel-Halls strike as the Malaysian side avenge their September loss and climb into second place.*

**News** · 2026-11-27

Kuching City FC moved into second place in Group H of the AFC Champions League Two on Thursday, November 26, defeating Cambodian champions Phnom Penh Crown 3-0 at Stadium Taha Ariffin in Kuching, Malaysia. Malaysian international Safawi Rasid opened the scoring in the 32nd minute, before second-half substitutes Jerome Mpacko Etame and Dylan Wenzel-Halls secured the victory to take head coach Aidil Sharin Sahak's side to seven points after five rounds.

## Safawi breaks the deadlock after early pressure

The hosts created early chances, striking the woodwork in the opening minute through an Ajdin Mujagić header before another Mujagić effort in the 11th minute was ruled out for offside. Kuching City controlled the first half with 62 percent possession and broke through at the 32-minute mark when Mujagić played a cross into the penalty box. Safawi controlled the ball past two Crown defenders and curled a left-footed shot past goalkeeper Um Vichet into the top corner. Phnom Penh Crown striker Moses Dyer forced Kuching goalkeeper Haziq Nadzli into a diving save six minutes before the break, preserving the home side's 1-0 advantage at halftime.

## Second-half substitutes punish the visitors

Phnom Penh Crown pushed forward early in the second half, but Kuching City doubled their margin in the 75th minute through an immediate impact from the bench. Seconds after replacing Safawi, Cameroonian forward Mpacko scored with his first touch of the match, finishing a low cross from Mujagić. In the 91st minute, Australian forward Wenzel-Halls collected a pass from Ahmad Sariweh, slipped past his marker, and drove a low shot into the net for the third goal.

The 3-0 result avenged Kuching City's 2-1 loss to Phnom Penh Crown at Morodok Techo National Stadium on September 17. "When we lost in Phnom Penh, we didn't deserve to lose," Aidil told the Sarawak Tribune. "Today proved that the boys want to show the quality they have. We don't need 11 players, we need everyone." Safawi, selected as Player of the Match, told reporters: "Tonight wasn't for me, but for the team."

## Group H permutations ahead of Matchday 6

Phnom Penh Crown remain at the top of Group H with nine points from five games despite the defeat. Kuching City climbed to second on seven points, leading South Korea's Gangwon FC on goal difference, while Hong Kong club Kitchee SC sit fourth on five points. The group concludes on December 3, when Kuching City travel to Hong Kong to face Kitchee and Phnom Penh Crown host Gangwon, with only the top two teams qualifying for the round of 16.
