# China Crush Fiji 8-0 to Clinch Historic U-17 World Cup Knockout Berth

*A dominant display in Doha sends the Chinese youth side into the Round of 32, ending a 21-year drought on the world stage.*

**News** · 2026-11-27

China secured qualification for the FIFA U-17 World Cup knockout stage on Thursday, 26 November 2026, defeating Fiji 8-0 at Pitch 3 of the Aspire Zone in Doha, Qatar. The victory sent Bin Ukishima's squad into the Round of 32 as one of the eight best third-placed teams from the 12 four-team groups.

The result marked only the third time in Chinese football history that the men's national under-17 side has advanced beyond the group phase at a World Cup, following quarter-final runs in 1985 on home soil and 2005 in Peru.

## First-half blitz at Aspire Zone

China took control inside three minutes when forward Zhao Songyuan converted a low pass across the face of goal for his third strike of the tournament. Fiji goalkeeper Savenaca Vatanitawake parried a Wan Xiang penalty in the 18th minute, but China doubled their lead four minutes later when Zhang Xuyao drove down the left wing and crossed for Kuang Zhaolei to finish.

Wan added the third in the 28th minute, slipping an acute-angle finish inside the near post after regaining possession in the attacking third. In the 32nd minute, Wan forced an own goal from Vatanitawake after rounding the goalkeeper, handing China a 4-0 advantage at half-time.

Kuang struck again in the 58th minute from Zhao's cutback before crossing for defender Zhang Junhao to nod home China's sixth in the 65th minute. Pan Chaowei set up Wan for an empty-net tap-in in the 72nd minute, and substitute Liang Shiyu completed the scoring in the 85th minute off Wan's through ball.

## Goal difference separates Group H runners-up

While China celebrated their biggest margin of victory in tournament history, they fell one goal short of automatic qualification as group runners-up. In the simultaneous Group H fixture, Spain defeated Morocco 2-0 to top the pool with nine points.

Morocco and China finished level on four points with identical 1-1-1 records. However, Morocco claimed second place on goal difference (+4 to China's +3), having beaten Fiji 6-0, drawn 2-2 with China, and lost 0-2 to Spain. China, who lost 0-5 to Spain on matchday one, pushed for a ninth goal in stoppage time, but a video review challenge for a penalty on Wan was turned down by the referee.

## Ending a 21-year drought

The qualification represents China's first appearance in a world tournament knockout round since the 2005 squad featuring Wang Dalei and Yu Dabao reached the last eight in South America. China's Round of 32 opponent will be confirmed on 28 November upon the conclusion of all 12 groups.
