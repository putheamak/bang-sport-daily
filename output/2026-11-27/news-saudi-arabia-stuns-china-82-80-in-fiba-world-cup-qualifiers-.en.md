# Saudi Arabia Stuns China 82-80 in FIBA World Cup Qualifiers for First Win in 33 Years

*A 16.7 percent shooting night from deep and Zhou Qi's ejection leave China at 5-4 in Group F.*

**News** · 2026-11-27

Saudi Arabia defeated China 82-80 on Thursday, November 26, in the fifth window of the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 Asian Qualifiers at Al Janoub Arena in Doha, Qatar. The result marked Saudi Arabia's first victory over China in an official FIBA contest since the 1993 Asian Basketball Championship in Jakarta.

## Zhou Qi ejection shifts momentum

China opened the game with a 10-2 run featuring three-pointers from Hu Mingxuan and Zeng Fanbo, holding a 29-25 lead after the first quarter. Saudi Arabia answered in the second period, outscoring China 23-14 to take a 48-43 advantage into halftime.

A pivotal moment occurred with 4:44 remaining in the third quarter when China center Zhou Qi was assessed a disqualifying foul for an elbow during interior positioning. Zhou was ejected after scoring 10 points on 5-of-6 shooting in 11 minutes. Saudi Arabia pushed the lead to eight before China trimmed the deficit to 68-66 at the end of the third quarter. In the fourth, guard Liao Sanning scored six straight points, and forward Li Hongquan sank three consecutive free throws to give China a 75-72 edge with 3:42 remaining.

## Shooting woes prove decisive

Saudi Arabia regained control during the final minutes. Forward Ali Shubayli sank two free throws with six seconds left to give Saudi Arabia an 82-79 lead. Hu made the first of two free throws with two seconds remaining, secured his own rebound after intentionally missing the second, but his buzzer-beating three-point attempt bounced off the rim.

China converted just 5 of 30 attempts from three-point range (16.7 percent), while Saudi Arabia went 7-for-23. The perimeter struggles offset China's 48-32 overall rebounding advantage, which included 25 offensive boards and a 30-11 edge in second-chance scoring.

Saudi center Mohammad Alsuwailem led all scorers with 20 points, eight rebounds, four assists, and two blocks. Five other Saudi players reached double figures, including Khaled Gabar with 15 points, Marzouq Almuwallad with 13, and Shubayli and Mohammad-Ali Abdur-Rahkman with 12 each. Liao and Hu led China with 15 points apiece, while Hu Jinqiu added 14 points and seven rebounds.

## Complications in Group F

"Poor three-point shooting was the key to our defeat," China head coach Guo Shiqiang told reporters. "We lost an important game and now we will face a more difficult task in securing World Cup qualification."

The loss dropped China to a 5-4 record in Group F, tied with South Korea, who hold the head-to-head tiebreaker after two earlier victories over China. Saudi Arabia moved to 4-5 to remain in contention for qualification. China next plays an away fixture against Lebanon on November 29.
