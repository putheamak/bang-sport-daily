# Italy Survives South Korea Scare to Reach Davis Cup Semifinals

*Matteo Berrettini and Andrea Vavassori win a deciding doubles thriller after world No. 139 Soonwoo Kwon stuns Flavio Cobolli.*

**News** · 2026-11-27

Three-time defending champion Italy held off an upset bid from South Korea on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, securing a 2-1 victory at BolognaFiere in Bologna to advance to the Davis Cup semifinals. Matteo Berrettini and Andrea Vavassori clinched the decisive doubles rubber, edging Soonwoo Kwon and Hyeon Chung 7-6 (7-2), 7-6 (13-11) after saving four second-set set points.

## Kwon upsets French Open finalist Cobolli

Lorenzo Musetti opened the quarterfinal tie by giving the hosts a 1-0 advantage. The world No. 27 needed 90 minutes to dispatch Hong Seong-chan, ranked 787th, 6-0, 7-6 (7-5).

South Korea leveled the contest in the second singles rubber through Kwon, who entered the match ranked 139th. The 28-year-old rallied past world No. 7 and 2026 French Open runner-up Flavio Cobolli 3-6, 7-6 (7-3), 7-6 (7-5) in two hours and 50 minutes. Kwon fell behind 3-1 in the third-set tiebreak before winning six of the final eight points to notch his sixth straight singles victory in Davis Cup play.

"We fought hard and I gave everything until the end," Cobolli told SuperTennis. "I played one of my best matches in recent times, but I came up against a player who is better than his current ranking."

## Marathon doubles rubber clinches tie

Kwon returned to the court 18 minutes after his singles win to team up with Chung, the 2018 Australian Open semifinalist. Neither pair surrendered a service break in the first set before Vavassori and Berrettini ran away with the tiebreak 7-2.

Both teams traded breaks in the second set to reach another tiebreak. South Korea overturned a 4-2 deficit to hold four set points, but Berrettini and Vavassori erased each opportunity and converted their third match point to end the contest after one hour and 54 minutes.

"I think I really enjoyed the game for the first time in a long time," Kwon said in his post-match press conference.

## Italy's streak reaches 15

The result marked Italy's 15th consecutive tie victory in the competition, a run that started during the 2023 finals group stage. Captain Filippo Volandri's squad will face Czechia in the semifinals on Friday, 27 November 2026, after the Czech team swept Canada 2-0 on Tuesday.

South Korea exited the tournament after reaching the Davis Cup quarterfinals for the first time since first entering the event in 1960. Despite entering the Final 8 as the only team without a player ranked inside the world's top 100, the South Korean squad forced four tiebreaks across the three rubbers against the host nation.
