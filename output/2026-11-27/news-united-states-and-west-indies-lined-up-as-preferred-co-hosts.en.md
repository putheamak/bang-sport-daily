# United States and West Indies Lined Up as Preferred Co-Hosts for 2034 Men's T20 World Cup

*The ICC plans a return to North America following the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics, proposing an expanded venue footprint across six US sites.*

**News** · 2026-11-27

The United States and the West Indies are being lined up to co-host the 2034 men's T20 World Cup, returning the tournament to North America a decade after their initial joint staging in 2024.

Following the International Cricket Council (ICC) board meetings in Bali held between 11 and 15 November 2026, the governing body approved marquee event schedules running through 2035. The 2034 T20 World Cup has been slated for July and August, a mid-year window that rules out Asian, African, and Oceanian venues due to seasonal monsoons and southern hemisphere winters. With the England and Wales Cricket Board ruling out a bid after securing hosting rights for the 2030 tournament alongside Ireland and Scotland, the American-Caribbean joint venture emerged as the ICC's favoured option.

## Expanding beyond the 2024 footprint

The ICC views North America as a critical growth region, seeking to build commercial momentum after cricket features as a medal sport at the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. 

During the 2024 edition, the United States staged 16 fixtures across three venues: Grand Prairie Stadium in Texas, Broward County Stadium in Florida, and a modular pop-up venue in Nassau County, New York. The West Indies hosted the remaining 39 matches. 

Preliminary plans for 2034 project a significantly more balanced allocation of matches between the two hosts. The domestic ground pool has expanded since 2024 through the growth of Major League Cricket and Olympic stadium preparations. Proposed venues include the temporary Olympic cricket ground in Pomona, California, Frederick Cricket Park near Washington D.C., the Oakland Coliseum in California, and Marymoor Park in Seattle, in addition to existing cricket infrastructure in Texas and Florida.

Cross-border cooperation between both administrations also continues at the bilateral level, with negotiations underway to stage two West Indies versus India T20 internationals across Florida and Washington D.C. in 2027.

## Governance hurdles and bankruptcy recovery

A formal award depends on resolving USA Cricket's governance and balance-sheet troubles. USA Cricket remains suspended by the ICC for breaches of membership criteria and financial mismanagement, having filed for bankruptcy protection in 2025.

The ICC has provided operational loans to maintain functioning staff, while the commercial backers of Major League Cricket contributed $3 million (£2.3 million) to fund a reorganization plan. Officials expect USA Cricket to normalize its status by 2027. The ICC will finalize its tournament bidding and allocation frameworks next year, with hosting rights for post-2031 tournaments scheduled to be awarded starting in 2028.
