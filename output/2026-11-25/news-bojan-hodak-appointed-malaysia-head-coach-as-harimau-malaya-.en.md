# Bojan Hodak Appointed Malaysia Head Coach as Harimau Malaya Reorganise

*The 55-year-old Croatian returns to Malaysian football as Tan Cheng Hoe transitions into the national team director role.*

**News** · 2026-11-25

PETALING JAYA — The Football Association of Malaysia (FAM) confirmed on Tuesday, November 24, 2026, the appointment of Bojan Hodak as the new head coach of the men's national team. The 55-year-old Croatian tactician succeeds interim head coach Tan Cheng Hoe, who took charge on June 25 following the departure of Peter Cklamovski. Cheng Hoe will now move into a full-time role as national team director.

## A Proven Regional Track Record

Hodak brings two decades of coaching experience across Southeast Asian football, particularly in Malaysia. His most celebrated domestic run occurred in 2012, when he guided Kelantan to a domestic treble by capturing the Malaysia Super League, the FA Cup, and the Malaysia Cup. 

He continued to accumulate domestic silverware across subsequent stints, steering Johor Darul Ta'zim to the 2014 Malaysia Super League title. Years later, he led Kuala Lumpur City FC to an unexpected triumph in the 2021 Malaysia Cup before guiding the capital club to the 2022 AFC Cup final, where they finished as runners-up.

Hodak also possesses previous experience within the national setup. Between 2017 and 2019, he managed the Malaysia Under-19 side, guiding them to victory at the 2018 AFF Under-19 Championship in Surabaya, Indonesia. Beyond Malaysia, Hodak collected league silverware in Cambodia with Phnom Penh Crown and spent time managing Indonesian clubs PSM Makassar and Persib Bandung.

## FAM Mandate and Strategic Direction

FAM president Datuk Ab Ghani Hassan explained that Hodak's deep familiarity with domestic players and regional competition made him the premier candidate for the role.

"FAM believes Hodak's extensive experience in Malaysian and Southeast Asian football will give him an advantage in understanding the local football environment and the potential of Malaysian players," Ab Ghani stated in an official announcement on Tuesday. "His track record with several clubs in Malaysia and Indonesia also demonstrates his ability to build competitive teams capable of vying for success."

With Cheng Hoe coordinating structural operations as director, Hodak's immediate focus turns to assembling Harimau Malaya's senior squad for upcoming regional matches and AFC Asian Cup qualification windows, aiming to stabilise performance and improve the nation's FIFA ranking.
