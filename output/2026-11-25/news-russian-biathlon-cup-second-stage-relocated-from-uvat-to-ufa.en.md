# Russian Biathlon Cup Second Stage Relocated from Uvat to Ufa Due to Lack of Snow

*The Russian Biathlon Union shifted the December event to Bashkortostan after unseasonably warm conditions left Tyumen Oblast without stable snow cover.*

**News** · 2026-11-25

The Russian Biathlon Union (RBU) announced on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, that the second stage of the 2026–27 Russian Biathlon Cup has been moved from the village of Uvat in Tyumen Oblast to Ufa, the capital of Bashkortostan.

The governing body confirmed the relocation after determining that the Uvat venue lacked the stable natural snow cover required to prepare competition tracks safely. Despite the sudden venue change, the event window remains intact, running from December 7 to December 14, 2026, with competitive race days scheduled from December 10 to December 13.

## Weather Forces Early Calendar Shift
A warmer start to the winter season across Western Siberia left organizers in Uvat unable to pack a durable snow base on the cross-country loops. With sub-zero temperatures failing to generate sufficient early snowfall in Tyumen Oblast, the RBU opted to move the stage west to avoid late postponements.

The second stage program includes men's and women's individual races, two sprint events, and mixed relay competitions. Ufa's biathlon complex, equipped with artificial snowmaking infrastructure and established trails, was cleared by federation officials to absorb the program without altering competition formats.

## Back-to-Back Stages in Bashkortostan
The relocation creates a rare two-week competitive hub in Bashkortostan. Ufa was already scheduled on the RBU calendar to host the third stage of the Russian Cup from December 14 to December 21, 2026. Competitors, coaching staffs, and wax teams will now remain at the same facility for consecutive cup meetings.

The dual-stage arrangement eliminates extensive mid-month travel for regional teams across Russia. Following the extended stop in Ufa, the circuit is scheduled to continue through winter stops before heading to Zlatoust for the Russian Cup finals from March 1 to March 8, 2027, where sprint, pursuit, and mass start disciplines will conclude the season.

## High Domestic Stakes for Banned Competitors
The domestic cup serves as the premier proving ground for Russia's elite biathletes, who have been suspended from International Biathlon Union (IBU) World Cup and World Championship events since early 2022. In the absence of international starts, national squad members compete almost exclusively across the Russian Cup circuit and the joint Russian-Belarusian Commonwealth Cup.

Preserving all scheduled race days is critical for federation points, athlete stipends, and national squad evaluations. By shifting directly to Ufa, the RBU ensures full racing mileage for both seasoned Olympians and emerging regional talent without disrupting the winter timetable.
