# Arthur Rinderknech Denies ITIA Anti-Doping Charge Over Alleged Test Refusal

*The French world No. 57 rejects the allegations on social media and heads to an independent tribunal without a provisional ban.*

**News** · 2026-11-25

French tennis player Arthur Rinderknech was formally charged by the International Tennis Integrity Agency (ITIA) on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, for allegedly refusing to submit to an anti-doping control. The 31-year-old world No. 57 responded on social media, issuing a complete denial and confirming that he will contest the accusation before an independent panel.

## Denial and Independent Tribunal Process

In a statement published on his Instagram account, Rinderknech expressed frustration that an inquiry meant to stay confidential had reached the media. He confirmed that he has formally contested the ITIA charge and exercised his right to have the dispute evaluated by an independent tribunal early in 2027.

The Frenchman asserted that he has never used prohibited substances and stated that presenting the full facts before arbitrators will result in his total exoneration. Following Rinderknech's public comments, the ITIA issued a statement confirming the charge. The governing body noted that it attempts to maintain confidentiality during ongoing proceedings and expressed disappointment that details entered the public domain prematurely.

Under tennis anti-doping regulations, an alleged test refusal does not carry a mandatory provisional suspension. Consequently, Rinderknech remains eligible to compete on the ATP Tour while awaiting his hearing.

## Severity of Sanctions and the Vondrousova Precedent

Under the World Anti-Doping Code, refusing or evading a sample collection without compelling justification carries the same baseline penalty as an adverse analytical finding: a four-year ban from all sanctioned competition.

The case mirrors that of 2023 Wimbledon champion Marketa Vondrousova. In June 2026, the Czech player was handed a four-year suspension by the ITIA after being found to have refused an anti-doping control. Vondrousova has since lodged an appeal with the Court of Arbitration for Sport in Lausanne, where proceedings remain pending.

## On-Court Context and Upcoming Schedule

Rinderknech achieved a career-high singles ranking of world No. 24 and has recorded notable results at the highest levels of the tour. In October 2025, he advanced to the final of the Shanghai Masters, an ATP Masters 1000 tournament, where he faced his cousin Valentin Vacherot.

His best Grand Slam result came at the 2025 US Open in New York, where he progressed to the fourth round of the men's singles draw. He also reached the third round at Wimbledon in both 2025 and 2026. Because no provisional suspension applies to his case, Rinderknech is eligible to enter tournaments at the start of the 2027 season, including the Australian Open preparation swing in January, ahead of his scheduled tribunal date.
