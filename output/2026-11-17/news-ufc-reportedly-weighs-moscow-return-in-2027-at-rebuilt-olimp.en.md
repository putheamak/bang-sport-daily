# UFC Reportedly Weighs Moscow Return in 2027 at Rebuilt Olimpiyskiy Complex

*State news agency TASS indicates mixed martial arts promotion is reviewing preliminary plans for its first event in the Russian capital in eight years.*

**News** · 2026-11-17

## A Potential Homecoming After Nearly a Decade

The Ultimate Fighting Championship could stage a live event in Moscow in 2027, according to reports published on November 16, 2026. Russian state news agency TASS reported that the global mixed martial arts organization is considering the recently reconstructed Olimpiyskiy sports complex as the prospective venue for the card.

If the promotion finalizes the schedule, the fixture would mark the organization's first return to the Russian capital in eight years. The promotion previously hosted UFC Fight Night 136 at the Olimpiyskiy Arena on September 15, 2018. That historical inaugural card was headlined by a heavyweight bout between Fabricio Werdum and Alexey Oleinik, drawing a passionate crowd in central Moscow.

A subsequent Russian event took place outside the capital at the Yubileyny Sports Palace in Saint Petersburg in April 2019, featuring Alistair Overeem against Alexey Oleinik, before international scheduling shifted away from the country in the following years.

## Venue Redevelopment and Regional Roster

The Olimpiyskiy sports complex, a prominent landmark in Moscow, has undergone extensive reconstruction during recent years. The large-scale renovation project transformed the older venue into a modern, multi-functional sports and entertainment complex designed to host major international competitions.

While the promotion has not yet issued an official calendar confirmation or fighter lineup for 2027, outlets including Blood and Sweat, Meta MMA, and Sport Den Za Diem highlighted the TASS reporting on preliminary discussions. The prospect of an event in Moscow signals a potential reopening of the Russian market for the organization, which features a substantial roster of athletes from the region competing across multiple weight divisions.

Fans and regional analysts await formal announcements from league executives regarding ticket sales, broadcast details, and the official 2027 fight calendar to confirm whether preliminary talks translate into a finalized event date.
