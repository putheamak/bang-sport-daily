# Former Akhmat Coach Oleg Kononov Criticizes Mid-Season Dismissals

*Oleg Kononov argues that Stanislav Cherchesov should have stayed in charge of the Grozny club, backing incoming manager Rolan Gusev for the future.*

**News** · 2026-11-17

## Mid-Season Stability in Focus

Former FC Akhmat manager Oleg Kononov has publicly criticized the practice of dismissing football coaches mid-season. In comments published on November 16, 2026, by Russian sports media outlets including Championat and Sports.ru, Kononov stated his opposition to removing coaching staff during an active campaign unless the manager steps down voluntarily.

Kononov addressed the recent managerial shifts at the Grozny-based club, asserting that retaining Stanislav Cherchesov would have yielded better outcomes. "Stanislav Salamovich is a respected specialist, and I am sure positive results would have arrived," Kononov told the publications.

## Transition to Rolan Gusev

Following Cherchesov's departure, the club appointed Rolan Gusev to lead the squad. Kononov offered words of encouragement for the new leadership while reflecting on his own ties to the team.

"What about Rolan Gusev, he has everything ahead of him. Very much would like Akhmat to win and show meaningful football under his leadership," Kononov stated.

## Managerial Turnover in the Russian Premier League

Coaching changes remain a frequent strategy among Russian football clubs seeking immediate improvements in domestic league standings. FC Akhmat has experienced multiple technical staff adjustments across various seasons in pursuit of higher placement in the Russian Premier League table. Kononov's remarks contribute to the ongoing discussion among Russian managers regarding the balance between institutional patience and the demand for rapid results.
