# Moritz Seider clarifies stance on potential Russia and Belarus return to World Championship

*The Germany defenseman addresses his recent remarks in an interview with Mannheimer Morgen, emphasizing his full backing of the German Ice Hockey Federation's position.*

**News** · 2026-11-17

## Clarification from the German Defenseman

Germany national team defenseman Moritz Seider has moved to clarify his previous remarks regarding the potential participation of Russia and Belarus at the 2027 IIHF World Championship. Speaking to *Mannheimer Morgen* in a report published on November 16, 2026, the 25-year-old blueliner addressed how his comments about wanting the world's best players on the ice were interpreted. Seider stated that he did not automatically advocate for the return of the two national teams to international competition.

In the interview, Seider explicitly aligned himself with the official stance of the German Ice Hockey Federation (DEB). "If my words did not sound the way I wanted them to or were misinterpreted, then I 100 percent support the position of the German Ice Hockey Federation and share the point of view that it voiced," Seider said, according to *Sports.ru*.

## Understanding the Nature of International Competition

Seider drew a distinct line between club competitions such as the National Hockey League, where players of various nationalities share a locker room to represent a single franchise, and international tournaments. He noted that the World Championship operates on a fundamentally different framework.

"As I said, the World Championship is a competition between countries, unlike the NHL, where we all play together for our club regardless of nationality," Seider explained. "I did not automatically advocate for Russia's return to the World Championship. I am well aware that the World Championship is not just a tournament in which the best players of a particular country participate, but precisely a competition between countries. That fundamentally changes the situation."

## Context of the 2027 Tournament

The discussion surrounds the upcoming 2027 IIHF World Championship, which is scheduled to take place in Germany in May 2027. Regarding the composition of the tournament, Seider had initially remarked that if Russia and Belarus participate, teams would welcome them, but if not, international events have functioned effectively in recent years.

The International Ice Hockey Federation has maintained restrictions on Russian and Belarus national teams from participating in its events following decisions made in response to geopolitical conflicts. The DEB has adhered strictly to these governance policies, prompting Seider's public reiteration of support for the governing body's official platform.
