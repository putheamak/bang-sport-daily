# Vietnam Scores First 11-a-Side World Cup Goals in Narrow 3-2 Loss to Belgium

*Audacious strikes from Lê Sỹ Bách and Chu Ngọc Nguyễn Lực deliver historic firsts for Vietnamese football at the FIFA U-17 World Cup in Qatar.*

**News** · 2026-11-21

Vietnam’s under-17 national football team made national history on Friday, November 20, scoring the country’s first goals at an 11-a-side FIFA tournament during a 3-2 defeat against Belgium in their opening Group G match at the Aspire Zone in Al Rayyan, Qatar.

Vietnam stunned the European runners-up after just four minutes when forward Lê Sỹ Bách met Nguyễn Minh Thủy’s near-post corner with an instinctive backheel flick past Belgian goalkeeper Mattis Seghers. Sven Vermant’s Belgium side leveled in the 39th minute through Tinus Moorthamer’s sharp strike from the right wing. Three minutes later, Kiyan Achahbar converted a cutback from Dierckx to give Belgium the lead heading into the break, before Anthony Philips extended the advantage to 3-1 on the hour mark.

## Historic Breakthrough on the World Stage

Vietnam mounted a late comeback in the 84th minute through Hanoi FC midfielder Chu Ngọc Nguyễn Lực, who curled an inswinging corner directly into the net off the post for an "Olimpico". The late strike set up a tense finish, though Belgian held on through added time officiated by Brazilian referee Daiane Muniz.

Prior to Friday's match, no Vietnamese team had ever scored in an 11-a-side FIFA finals tournament. Vietnam's men's Under-20 team in 2017 and the senior women's national team in 2023 both competed in World Cup group stages without finding the net. While Vietnam previously scored in the 2016 and 2021 FIFA Futsal World Cups, the goals by Lê Sỹ Bách and Chu Ngọc Nguyễn Lực marked the breakthrough on the full-pitch world stage.

## Reactions and Group G Outlook

"Belgium are a very strong team and operate at a different level from us. But we prepared well and managed to score two beautiful goals," said Vietnam head coach Cristiano Roland.

Belgian manager Vermant praised his team's composure after the early deficit. "I have to admit it was a very tense match. The most important thing is that Belgium found a way to score and win, taking three points from our World Cup opener," Vermant said. Belgian goalscorer Philips noted that while Vietnam started sharply, Belgium maintained control to see out the result.

The 2026 FIFA U-17 World Cup in Qatar represents the first edition of the tournament expanded to 48 teams divided into 12 groups. Vietnam is one of eight debutant nations in the competition alongside Greece, Montenegro, Mozambique, Romania, Serbia, Tanzania, and the Dominican Republic. Roland’s squad will next face New Zealand and Mali in Group G at the Aspire Zone, while Belgium meets Mali.
