# Vietnam Push Belgium to the Brink in Spirited 3-2 U-17 World Cup Debut

*Wonder goals from Le Sy Bach and Chu Ngoc Nguyen Luc cannot prevent a narrow opening defeat to the European runners-up in Doha*

**News** · 2026-11-21

Vietnam fell to a 3-2 defeat against European runners-up Belgium in their opening Group G match of the 2026 FIFA U-17 World Cup at the Aspire Zone in Doha on Friday, November 20. The encounter was Vietnam's first-ever match at the FIFA U-17 World Cup, with striker Le Sy Bach and midfielder Chu Ngoc Nguyen Luc delivering two eye-catching strikes that kept the European side under pressure until the final whistle.

## Early Audacity at the Aspire Zone

Vietnam head coach Cristiano Roland had instructed his squad before kickoff to approach their historic debut with self-belief and composure. "Against Belgium U17, I just want the players to show their best and give everything they have," Roland told reporters ahead of the match. "Whatever the result, when the match ends, the entire team will be proud of what they have done."

His squad delivered an immediate response within four minutes of kickoff. Following a low corner taken from the left flank by winger Nguyen Minh Thuy, forward Le Sy Bach darted toward the near post and redirected the ball past Belgian goalkeeper Mattis Seghers with an audacious backheel flick. The clever finish made Sy Bach the first Vietnamese player to score at an 11-a-side FIFA World Cup finals.

## Belgium Fight Back Before the Break

Sven Vermant's Belgian side, who reached Qatar as runners-up at the 2026 UEFA European Under-17 Championship and brought a squad with an average height of 1.84 metres compared to Vietnam's 1.75 metres, gradually took control of possession. Belgium pulled level in the 39th minute when defender Tinus Moorthamer drove a sharp strike from a tight angle past goalkeeper Ly Xuan Hoa. Three minutes later, Belgian playmaker Xander Dierckx surged down the right touchline and played a low cutback for Kiyan Achahbar, who tapped home from close range to give Belgium a 2-1 halftime lead.

Belgium extended their advantage to 3-1 on the hour mark when Anthony Philips pounced on a loose pass in midfield, raced clear through the centre, and tucked the ball past Xuan Hoa. Vietnam refused to capitulate and found another breakthrough in the 84th minute. Midfielder Chu Ngoc Nguyen Luc stepped up to a corner on the left and curled the ball directly inside the far post for an "olímpico" goal, setting up a frantic stoppage time in which Belgium clung to their one-goal cushion.

## Group G Standings and What Comes Next

The 3-2 scoreline leaves Belgium level on three points with Mali, who defeated Oceania champions New Zealand 1-0 in the other Group G fixture. Under the expanded 48-team tournament format, the top two teams from each of the 12 groups qualify for the round of 32, alongside the eight best third-placed finishers. Vietnam face New Zealand next in Doha on Monday, November 23, before concluding their group stage against Mali on November 26.
