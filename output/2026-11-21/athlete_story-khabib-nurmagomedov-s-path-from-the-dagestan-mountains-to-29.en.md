# Khabib Nurmagomedov's Path From the Dagestan Mountains to 29-0 MMA Perfection

*Under the lifelong instruction of his father Abdulmanap, the lightweight champion dominated the UFC before fulfilling a promise to retire at his peak.*

**Athlete story** · 2026-11-21

Khabib Abdulmanapovich Nurmagomedov concluded his mixed martial arts career with an undefeated professional record of 29 wins and zero losses, reigning as UFC lightweight champion for 1,134 days from April 2018 until March 2021. His resume includes eight knockouts, 11 submissions, 10 decisions, and a formal induction into the UFC Hall of Fame on June 30, 2022. Born on September 20, 1988, in the mountain village of Sildi in the Tsumadinsky District of Dagestan, Nurmagomedov became the first Russian and first Muslim to capture an undisputed UFC world title. His dominance inside the cage rested on combat sambo techniques engineered by his father, Abdulmanap Nurmagomedov, an athlete who built a training system in the North Caucasus that produced 18 world sambo champions.

Nurmagomedov grew up in an Avar family alongside his older brother, Magomed, and younger sister, Amina. When his family relocated from Sildi to the village of Kirovaul, his father converted the ground floor of their two-story home into a wrestling gym. Abdulmanap, a Soviet Army veteran decorated in freestyle wrestling and sambo, began training Khabib in wrestling at age eight. By age nine, Khabib's training regime included wrestling a chained bear cub, captured on home video in 1997. In 2001, the family moved to Dagestan's capital, Makhachkala, where Khabib intensified wrestling training at 12, took up judo under his father at 15 to adapt to competing in a gi jacket, and added combat sambo at 17.

Before entering professional mixed martial arts, Nurmagomedov captured two World Combat Sambo championships under the World Combat Sambo Federation. He made his professional MMA debut on September 13, 2008, winning four bouts in less than four weeks. On October 11, 2008, he won three fights in one night in Moscow to secure the inaugural Atrium Cup tournament championship. Over the next three years, he compiled a 16–0 regional record across Russia and Ukraine, finishing 11 of 12 opponents before the UFC offered him a contract in late 2011 following the resolution of an 11-case promotional dispute with ProFC.

## The American Kickboxing Academy and UFC Records

Nurmagomedov made his promotional debut on January 20, 2012, at UFC on FX 1 in Nashville, submitting Kamal Shalorus with a rear-naked choke in the third round. Six months later, on July 7, 2012, at UFC 148 in Las Vegas, he earned a unanimous decision over Gleison Tibau in his first bout prepared at the American Kickboxing Academy in San Jose, California, under head coach Javier Mendez. On May 25, 2013, at UFC 160, Nurmagomedov defeated Abel Trujillo by unanimous decision while setting the UFC record for the most takedowns in a single fight, landing 21 of 28 attempts. He followed that performance on April 19, 2014, by dominating future champion Rafael dos Anjos across three rounds at UFC on Fox 11 in Orlando.

Injuries halted Nurmagomedov's momentum between 2014 and 2016. Successive knee injuries and rib fractures forced cancellations of scheduled bouts against Donald Cerrone and Tony Ferguson, keeping him out of action for two years until he stopped Darrell Horcher on April 16, 2016, in Tampa. He reasserted his lightweight status on November 12, 2016, at UFC 205 at Madison Square Garden by submitting Michael Johnson with a third-round kimura, followed by a lopsided decision over Edson Barboza at UFC 219 on December 30, 2017.

## Championship Dominance and the McGregor Rivalry

On April 7, 2018, at UFC 223 inside Brooklyn's Barclays Center, Nurmagomedov claimed the vacant UFC lightweight belt by defeating Al Iaquinta via unanimous decision with scores of 50–44, 50–43, and 50–43. His first title defense on October 6, 2018, at UFC 229 against Conor McGregor set a combat-sports record with 2.4 million pay-per-view buys. Nurmagomedov submitted McGregor with a neck crank at 3:03 of round four. Immediately following the tap, Nurmagomedov jumped the Octagon fence into McGregor's corner, sparking a brawl that led the Nevada State Athletic Commission to issue him a nine-month suspension and a $500,000 fine.

Nurmagomedov returned to the cage on September 7, 2019, at UFC 242 in Abu Dhabi, unifying the lightweight crown by submitting interim champion Dustin Poirier with a rear-naked choke at 2:06 of round three. That bout marked the only time Abdulmanap cornered his son in a UFC championship fight, due to prior United States visa denials. Across 13 UFC bouts, Nurmagomedov lost only two individual rounds on official judges' scorecards—the third round against McGregor and the first round against Justin Gaethje.

## The Final Promise and Life After Competition

On July 3, 2020, Abdulmanap Nurmagomedov died at a Moscow hospital at age 57 from complications linked to COVID-19. Three months later, on October 24, 2020, Nurmagomedov entered the cage without his father for UFC 254 on Fight Island in Abu Dhabi against interim champion Justin Gaethje. Nurmagomedov finished the bout at 1:34 of the second round with a mounted triangle choke, knelt in the center of the canvas in tears, and removed his gloves. During his post-fight interview, he announced his retirement at age 32: "Ain't no way I'm going to come here without my father. When UFC called me about Justin, I talked with my mother three days. She didn't want me to fight without father, but I promised her it was going to be my last fight."

UFC president Dana White confirmed Nurmagomedov's official title vacation on March 19, 2021. Moving outside the cage, Nurmagomedov bought the Russian promotion Gorilla Fighting Championship for $1 million, rebranding it as Eagle Fighting Championship (EFC). He also served as head cornerman and coach for American Kickboxing Academy teammates, guiding Islam Makhachev to the UFC lightweight championship in October 2022 alongside his cousins Umar and Usman Nurmagomedov. In January 2023, Nurmagomedov announced his withdrawal from travel and corner duties to spend his time with his family in Dagestan.
