# Russian MMA Fighter Amir Aliev Released After Paris Detention

*The 27-year-old bantamweight was freed without charge following police questioning over an alleged incident in Montreuil.*

**News** · 2026-11-23

Russian mixed martial arts fighter Amir Aliev was released from police custody in France on Sunday, November 22, 2026, two days after being detained in Paris following his bout at ARES 45. The 27-year-old bantamweight had been taken into custody on Friday night, November 20, at the Adidas Arena in Paris, where French police acted on an investigation into a rape complaint. Speaking to Russian state news agency TASS on Sunday, Aliev confirmed that he was freed without charge, stating that authorities recognized the allegations as groundless.

## Detention following ARES 45 victory

The detention unfolded moments after Aliev earned a unanimous decision victory over Brazilian opponent Matheus Malta at the Adidas Arena in the 61-kilogram division. Around 10:30 p.m. local time on November 20, officers approached Aliev inside the venue. According to reports from French newspaper Le Parisien, Aliev complied without resistance and was escorted to a police commissariat in Montreuil, located in the Seine-Saint-Denis department east of Paris.

The Bobigny prosecutor's office confirmed that investigators placed him under garde à vue, a French legal framework enabling temporary police detention for questioning. The complaint under scrutiny pertained to an alleged incident reported to have occurred in Montreuil several days prior to the fight. French custody rules permit an initial detention period of up to 48 hours for questioning, which was extended on November 22 before prosecutors allowed the window to conclude without issuing formal charges.

## Fighter's response and promotional career

Aliev stated that the complaint originated from a verbal altercation earlier in the week. "It is a lie, and such a thing on my part is completely excluded," Aliev told TASS. "The French police acknowledged that a mistake was made and there were no grounds to accuse me." Aliev added that members of his corner, including his manager, had also been questioned before being released alongside him. While Aliev stated that investigators closed the matter, French judicial officials had not issued a formal public statement regarding the administrative status of the case by Monday, November 23.

Born in Grozny, Russia, Aliev spent the majority of his professional mixed martial arts career competing in Russian circuits, including ACA Young Eagles and Hardcore MMA. He transitioned to the French-based Ares Fighting Championship in 2026. The unanimous decision over Malta marked his third consecutive promotional victory under the ARES banner, keeping his European campaign intact following his release.
