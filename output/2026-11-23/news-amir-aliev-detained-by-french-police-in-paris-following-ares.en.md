# Amir Aliev Detained by French Police in Paris Following ARES 45 Victory

*The 27-year-old Russian bantamweight was taken into custody at Adidas Arena under an investigation into an alleged sexual assault.*

**News** · 2026-11-23

Russian mixed martial artist Amir Aliev was detained by French police on Friday, 20 November 2026, moments after winning his bantamweight bout at the ARES 45 event inside the Adidas Arena in Paris.

Aliev had defeated Brazil's Matheus Malta by unanimous decision over three rounds before officers intercepted him backstage at approximately 10:30 p.m.. According to French daily *Le Parisien*, the 27-year-old fighter offered no resistance when approached by law enforcement personnel within the arena premises.

## Custody at Montreuil and the Bobigny probe

Following his arrest, Aliev was transported to a police station in the eastern Paris suburb of Montreuil, located in the Seine-Saint-Denis department. French authorities placed him under *garde à vue*—a preliminary police custody measure permitted under French criminal procedure for questioning and evidence gathering.

The Bobigny public prosecutor's office confirmed that Aliev's custody was extended on Saturday, 21 November 2026, allowing investigators additional time to review the complaint. The inquiry stems from a rape complaint filed by an individual alleging that an assault occurred in Montreuil several days prior to Friday's fight card. Under French law, the opening of a preliminary custody period does not constitute a formal criminal charge or a finding of guilt, and Aliev retains the presumption of innocence while the investigation proceeds. Neither Aliev nor his legal representatives have issued a public statement regarding the allegations.

## Card context and sporting record

Aliev was born in Grozny, Chechnya, and entered Friday's contest with prior competitive appearances in Russian organizations including ACA Young Eagles and Hardcore MMA. The three-round decision over Malta moved his mixed martial arts record to 13 victories and two defeats.

The bout took place on the preliminary portion of ARES 45, an event that drew a sell-out crowd of 8,000 spectators to the Adidas Arena. The evening's headline fight featured Baysangur "Baki" Chamsoudinov reclaiming the ARES welterweight championship from Jordan Zebo via a five-round unanimous decision. Organizers of the ARES Fighting Championship have not released an official comment regarding the police action inside the venue.
