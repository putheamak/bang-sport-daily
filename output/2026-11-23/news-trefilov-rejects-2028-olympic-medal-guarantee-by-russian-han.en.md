# Trefilov Rejects 2028 Olympic Medal Guarantee by Russian Handball Chief

*The 2016 Rio gold medal coach cautions against bold podium pledges as officials pursue a wild card path to Los Angeles.*

**News** · 2026-11-23

Former Russian women's national handball coach Yevgeni Trefilov dismissed public guarantees of an Olympic podium finish at Los Angeles 2028, telling newspaper Sport-Express on Sunday, 22 November 2026, that "money loves silence, and victories do too". His comments directly addressed statements made two days earlier by Sergey Shishkarev, chairman of the Supreme Council of the Handball Federation of Russia (HFR), who vowed that the national squad would medal when the Games take place in July 2028.

"The chances are always 50-50. I wouldn't get so hot-headed," the 71-year-old Trefilov said to Sport-Express. "Everything should be done quietly. As they say, money loves silence, and victories do too. Of course, I would like to see our national team on the podium all the time. It is very pleasant and warms the soul".

## Contrasting Visions at Moscow Film Premiere

The exchange followed the premiere of the documentary film *Miracle in Rio* (*Chudo v Rio*), directed by Nikolai Akelkin, held in Moscow on Friday, 20 November 2026. The documentary chronicles Russia’s run to the gold medal at the 2016 Rio de Janeiro Olympics, where Trefilov coached the squad to a 22-19 victory over France in the final after an overtime semifinal win against Norway featuring decisive penalty conversions by Ekaterina Ilyina.

Speaking at the screening, Shishkarev projected a return to the Olympic rostrum: "There is a specific date — July 2028, the city is Los Angeles. I promise you that the Russian national team will stand on the podium there". Trefilov struck a more reserved tone from the same stage on Friday. "We talk a lot and do little. Victory is just a moment. After a victory, you need to go back to the gym for another second of victory," Trefilov said. "I would like handball to be treated a little differently. Our girls showed with their results at the Olympics that handball is not the last sport for Russia".

## The Hurdles Facing Los Angeles 2028

Russia captured women's handball gold at Rio 2016 and followed it with silver at Tokyo 2020, but the national side has not contested an Olympic fixture since August 2021 following international bans from world and European competitions instituted in 2022. The 2028 Summer Olympic Games in Los Angeles are scheduled to run from 14 to 30 July 2028, leaving a narrow qualification window.

To establish a qualification route, Russian officials are seeking entry into the 2027 IHF Women's World Championship. Shishkarev confirmed plans to hold talks with International Handball Federation (IHF) president Hassan Moustafa to request a wild card invitation for the Russian team, which would allow them to compete for the Olympic tournament berths awarded at the event.
