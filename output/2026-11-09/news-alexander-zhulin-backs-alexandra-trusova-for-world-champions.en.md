# Alexander Zhulin Backs Alexandra Trusova for World Championship Podium After Shenzhen Grand Prix

*Following a silver-medal finish at the Grand Prix event in China, the veteran coach predicts further technical upgrades and global contention in Tampere.*

**News** · 2026-11-09

The international figure skating season delivered a compelling test of form over the weekend as the Grand Prix series stop in Shenzhen concluded on Sunday, November 9, 2026. Japanese skater Mone Chiba captured the gold medal after a closely contested field, while Russian competitor Alexandra Trusova secured the silver medal in a performance that drew widespread attention from the sport's coaching elite.

Following the conclusion of the event in southern China, honoured Russian figure skating coach Alexander Zhulin offered a strong endorsement of Trusova's competitive trajectory. "Magnificent skating, she is a great credit! Soon she will be taking first places," Zhulin told Russian media outlets, highlighting the quality of her programs on the international stage.

## Shenzhen Podium and the Path to Finland

The Grand Prix circuit serves as a vital proving ground for athletes navigating international panels and pacing their physical peaks toward the global championships. Zhulin pointed out that continued adaptation to judging standards and potential technical additions will define the coming months. "It seems to me that she will add another quadruple jump, improve in her skating, and the judges will get used to her," Zhulin said. Looking ahead to the major global gathering, he added, "Regarding first place at the World Championships I do not know, but she should be in the top three."

The upcoming World Figure Skating Championships are scheduled to take place in March 2027 in the Finnish city of Tampere. The event will bring together the world's leading singles skaters to contest global titles on European ice.

## Context on International Competition

The Shenzhen Grand Prix stop highlighted the intense competitive pressures present on the senior international circuit. Alongside the high-level technical displays on the ice, the event underscored the passionate engagement of global fanbases across digital platforms, reflecting the intense scrutiny top competitors face as the sport moves deeper into the 2026-2027 calendar.

Coaches and analysts will closely monitor how athletes adjust their layout structures and jump content during the remaining Grand Prix assignments before domestic selection events and the spring championships in Tampere.
