# SKA forward Vasily Glotov sets sights on Russian national team return

*The Kontinental Hockey League forward discusses international ambitions and the motivation provided by potential global tournaments.*

**News** · 2026-11-11

## International ambitions in St. Petersburg

Vasily Glotov, the forward for Kontinental Hockey League club SKA Saint Petersburg, stated on November 11, 2026, that earning a spot on the Russian national team for upcoming world championships serves as a major career driver.

In an interview published by Championat and Sports.ru, the 29-year-old forward emphasized that the prospect of representing his country on the international stage provides significant personal motivation during the club season.

## Career context and club performance

Glotov addressed the broader landscape of international competition while maintaining focus on his on-ice performance for SKA. The forward has established himself as a key contributor in Saint Petersburg, drawing attention from analysts tracking national team selection.

Reporting from Championat and Sports.ru confirmed his remarks, which highlighted his commitment to athletic goals amid ongoing discussions surrounding international sports governance and tournament participation.

The forward's focus remains on maintaining high club form to secure a roster position whenever international fixtures resume for the national setup.
