FC Spartak Moscow welcome FC Akron Togliatti to the capital on Saturday, November 8, 2026, for a Russian Premier League fixture. The league encounter at the Lukoil Arena is scheduled to begin at 16:30 Moscow time.

Both clubs entered the fixture looking to solidify their positions in the upper and middle tiers of the Russian top-flight standings. Spartak continue their campaign under high expectations from their fan base, aiming to maintain pressure on the division's front-runners.

Meanwhile, Akron Togliatti approach the match seeking valuable points on the road as they establish themselves in the elite division following their historic promotion. Fixtures against traditional heavyweights like Spartak provide a major measuring stick for the provincial side.
