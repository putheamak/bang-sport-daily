# Russian National Team Claims 21 Gold Medals at IMMAF World Championships in Tbilisi

*The global mixed martial arts tournament concluded in Georgia, with the Russian squad dominating the gold medal count across divisions.*

**News** · 2026-11-08

The Russian national mixed martial arts team secured 21 gold medals at the International Mixed Martial Arts Federation (IMMAF) World Championships, which concluded on November 8, 2026, in Tbilisi, Georgia.

## Dominance on the Global Stage

The tournament brought together elite amateur and youth fighters from around the globe to compete under the auspices of the IMMAF. The Russian delegation established a commanding presence throughout the event, topping the podium in multiple weight categories and demonstrating exceptional technical depth across both men's and women's divisions.

## Context and Significance

The IMMAF World Championships remain the premier proving ground for developing mixed martial arts talent before athletes transition to professional promotions. Performances at this level often signal the arrival of future stars in the sport. The capital of Georgia served as a bustling hub for international combat sports during the competition week, welcoming hundreds of competitors, coaches, and officials.

## What Comes Next

With the global championships concluded, national federations will shift their focus to continental tournaments and domestic calendars for the upcoming season. Meanwhile, standout medalists from Tbilisi are expected to draw attention from major professional organizations as they weigh the transition to the paid ranks.
