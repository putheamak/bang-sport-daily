# Mishina and Galliamov seal triumphant return with gold at Grand Prix of China

*Anastasia Mishina and Aleksandr Galliamov captured the pairs title in China, reaffirming their status among the world's elite after a long absence from international competition.*

**News** · 2026-11-10

Anastasia Mishina and Aleksandr Galliamov secured the gold medal in pairs figure skating at the ISU Grand Prix of China, delivering a commanding performance upon their return to major international competition. The victory marks a significant milestone for the accomplished duo, who had previously claimed the world championship title before international sports bans suspended participation by Russian athletes.

## A Seamless Return to the Top

Reflecting on the victory, choreographer and coach Betina Popova praised the pair's execution under pressure. "Our guys showed the maximum," Popova told Sports.ru, emphasizing that their return reestablishes them firmly on the position of the best. "Do ostraneniya rossijskih sportsmenov ot mezhdunarodnyh startov kak raz-taki Anastasiya s Aleksandrom vyigrali chempionat mira, i vot oni vernulis' i vse tak zhe na pozitsii luchshih, kak eto i dolzhno byt'," she added, noting the emotional significance of the triumph.

## The Broader Competitive Landscape

Popova also highlighted the wider state of disciplines such as men's and pairs skating, suggesting that intense domestic competition during the period of international isolation helped skaters maintain rigorous technical standards. According to analysts, the depth of talent within these categories has allowed athletes to deliver clean, polished routines capable of capturing high scores upon returning to the global circuit.

## Looking Ahead on the Grand Prix Circuit

As the ISU Grand Prix series continues, attention turns to subsequent stages and how returning competitors will measure against emerging international challengers. For Mishina and Galliamov, claiming the top step of the podium in China validates years of consistent training regimes and sets a clear benchmark for the remainder of the season.
