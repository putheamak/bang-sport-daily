# Russian judoka Mikhailin reacts to Teddy Riner's French presidential bid

*London 2012 silver medalist Alexander Mikhailin has offered words of support to French Olympic champion Teddy Riner after the judo legend announced his candidacy for the French presidency.*

**News** · 2026-11-10

## A former rival's perspective

Russian judo veteran Alexander Mikhailin has publicly wished success to five-time Olympic champion Teddy Riner following the French athlete's announcement that he will run for the presidency of France. 

Mikhailin, who secured a silver medal at the London 2012 Olympic Games after facing Riner in the heavyweight final, shared his thoughts on his former opponent's political ambitions. The 37-year-old French judo star declared his intention to enter the political arena, prompting reactions from international sports figures.

"If he has decided to choose such a path, is genuinely running, and gets elected, I wish him success," Mikhailin told Russian media. He noted that Riner carries significant backing in France, particularly after capturing gold at the Paris 2024 Olympic Games.

## The intersection of sport and politics

Mikhailin emphasized Riner's status as a role model for French youth, pointing out the judoka's independence from traditional political parties. At the same time, the former Russian competitor raised diplomatic considerations regarding future international relations.

"To ensure he keeps his word and sets tasks that are truly acceptable and necessary for his country," Mikhailin added, expressing hope that friendly ties between France and Russia could be maintained.

The announcement of Riner's presidential aspirations marks an unprecedented transition from elite combat sports into the highest echelons of national politics. While athletic champions occasionally move into sports administration or local governance, a direct bid for a national presidency remains a rare path.

## Looking ahead to the campaign

As Riner prepares for the electoral campaign, sports analysts and political observers continue to evaluate how his immense popularity on the tatami will translate into the political sphere. Neither French sports authorities nor international Olympic bodies have released official statements regarding the campaign.

The progression of Riner's political movement will unfold over the coming months as the candidate outlines his platform for the French electorate.
