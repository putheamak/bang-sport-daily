# Voice-over script: Lionel Messi life story (published 27 Nov 2026). Facts only from the published story.
# About 45 seconds. Lines starting with # are not read.
Forty-seven trophies. More than nine hundred and thirty goals. And it all began with a paper napkin.

This is Bang Sport Daily.

Lionel Messi was born in Rosario, Argentina, in 1987. At ten, doctors found a growth hormone deficiency, and his treatment cost the family at least one thousand pesos a month.

At thirteen, Barcelona's Carles Rexach wrote him an agreement on a paper napkin.

What followed: thirty-four trophies with Barcelona, six hundred and seventy-two goals for the club, and eight Ballons d'Or.

In 2022, he finally lifted the World Cup with Argentina.

On the sixth of October 2026, he played his farewell match for his country, a three-nil win over Benin. He finished with two hundred and eight caps and one hundred and twenty-six goals.

The full story is on bangsportdaily.com.
