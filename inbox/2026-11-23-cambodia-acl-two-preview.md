Cambodia's two clubs in the AFC Champions League Two go into Thursday's fifth round of group matches, on 26 November, in very different moods. Preah Khan Reach Svay Rieng top the Cambodian Premier League (CPL) after a 5-0 win over Angkor Tiger on Sunday. Phnom Penh Crown lead their Asian group but sit eighth at home after a 1-1 draw with Kirivong Sok Sen Chey on Saturday.

With two group matches left for each side, both clubs can still reach the Round of 16 in February.

## Svay Rieng: in form at the right time

Svay Rieng have scored nine goals and conceded one in four days. On 19 November they beat Thailand's Port FC 4-1 in the ASEAN Club Championship, with goals from Min Ratanak, Martins Júnior, Tiago Alves and Kwame Peprah. On Sunday they beat Angkor Tiger 5-0 in front of 7,502 spectators, more than three-quarters of the 9,789 who watched the five Week 8 matches.

The three-time defending champions are unbeaten after seven league games (five wins, two draws), with the league's best attack (22 goals) and best defence (5 conceded). They lead on 17 points, level with Nagaworld, who have played a game more.

In Group G of the AFC Champions League Two, Svay Rieng are second on 7 points. They lost 2-0 away to Japan's FC Machida Zelvia, drew 0-0 at home with Shanghai Shenhua, won 5-1 away to Singapore's Tampines Rovers and beat Tampines 2-0 at home.

Machida, who have won all four games, now visit Cambodia on Thursday. The top two in each group go through. Svay Rieng lead Shenhua by three points, and the two sides meet in the final round, so any point against Machida would take Svay Rieng into that last match with an advantage. The one concern is fatigue: Thursday is their third match in eight days.

## Phnom Penh Crown: strong in Asia, struggling at home

Crown are a different team on each stage. In Group H they lead with 9 points from four games: a 2-1 win over Malaysia's Kuching City, a 2-1 win away to Korea's Gangwon FC, a 2-0 home win over Hong Kong's Kitchee and a 2-1 defeat in the return match in Hong Kong. Kitchee are second on 5 points, with Kuching and Gangwon on 4.

A win in Kuching on Thursday would give Crown 12 points, more than any other team in the group can reach, and confirm first place with a game to spare.

At home, the record eight-time champions have one league win in seven matches (one win, four draws, two defeats) and sit eighth on 7 points, ten behind Svay Rieng. Their only victory was 2-1 away to the Royal Cambodian Armed Forces on 24 October. In November they lost 1-0 to MOI Kompong Dewa and 2-1 to Boeung Ket before Saturday's draw with Kirivong, who are tenth. With only the top two earning continental places for next season, Crown cannot let the gap grow much further.

## The title race: Nagaworld and Visakha keep pace

**Nagaworld** have matched the leaders on points. A 1-0 win over ISI Dangkor Senchey on Saturday took them level with Svay Rieng on 17 points from eight games. Their only league defeat was a 3-1 loss at Svay Rieng on 8 November, and their goal difference (+4) is far smaller than the leaders' (+17), showing how many of their wins have been narrow.

**Visakha**, seventh last season, are third on 16 points from seven games and had no match in Week 8. They have scored 19 goals, second only to Svay Rieng. Andrés Nieto, the Colombian-born Cambodia international who joined from Crown, scored twice in a 6-1 win away to the Royal Cambodian Armed Forces. Visakha drew 2-2 at Svay Rieng on 24 October, and their only defeat was a 3-1 loss at Angkor Tiger on 8 November.

MOI Kompong Dewa are fourth on 13 points after a 2-1 win on Sunday, with the league's second-best defence (5 conceded in seven games).

## At the bottom: no points for the Royal Cambodian Armed Forces

The Royal Cambodian Armed Forces (National Defense), who reverted to that name in 2025, have lost all seven league games. They have scored 7 goals and conceded 29. Their defeats include 4-0 at Svay Rieng, 6-0 at Angkor Tiger and 6-1 at home to Visakha. The closest they came was a 5-4 home defeat to Nagaworld in September. Sunday's 2-1 home loss to MOI Kompong Dewa left them eight points behind tenth-placed Kirivong Sok Sen Chey.
